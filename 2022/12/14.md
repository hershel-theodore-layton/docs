# 2022-12-14

## Highlights

- HHVM's binary and compact Thrift serializers now apply parameterized type wrappers, including wrappers nested in container fields. Generated wrapped-field setters are used where nullable fields do not reveal the wrapper class at runtime. [2416be18d2](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/2416be18d2661587ec5f9db0ba13c2ade5a47ef3)
- The bundled `HH\TypeStructure` declaration now types `param_types` and `elem_types` as `?varray<mixed>` instead of leaving their legacy-array generic arguments unspecified. [56002867f6](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/56002867f6295336085e126c950e3269e2035d37) [15721deb25](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/15721deb25cffe8c1c6f648f3084ad362b9ff41f)
