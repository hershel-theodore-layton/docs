# 2023-01-04

## Highlights

- Memoized functions and methods may now use `<<__Memoize(#Uncategorized)>>` or its `__MemoizeLSB` equivalent. This has ordinary memoization behavior but records the explicit category in reflection. Functions whose contexts cannot access implicit context must continue to omit a memoization option. [e2bc6db35d](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e2bc6db35d4669e3870e9f2c1bb5507c8fd5dbad)
- Hover information for functions and methods that support dynamic now prints the source-level `<<__SupportDynamicType>>` attribute instead of exposing its internal `supportdyn<...>` representation. [df90d157ec](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/df90d157ec9214370794203146003c84428df05f)
