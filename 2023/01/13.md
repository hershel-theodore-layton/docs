# 2023-01-13

## Highlights

- Missing and extra shape-field errors now point at the individual field, and a missing field in a shape literal offers a quick-fix that inserts a `TODO` value. [5f0a767ff1](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5f0a767ff1df407b782a576cae11676b2ef639a4) [bbff846c01](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/bbff846c010c54a31b35cb24d5f1be07727a3d20)
- HHI declarations now type the output of `parse_str()` and the arrays passed to `Collator::sort()` and `Collator::asort()` as `dynamic`, accurately representing their heterogeneous results. [083c6643ef](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/083c6643efdb1eb7fa4bea57811d188330b90496) [834a393008](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/834a393008a212ec8c6796016dcaddc7d41e0259)
- Runtime type-structure helpers now compare the `alias` and `kind` fields case-sensitively, like other shape keys. [16acff3a5c](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/16acff3a5cdc8bffc35359a58ef83104c9be1018)
- Only the exactly cased method name `__construct` is treated as a constructor by runtime method-name checks. A differently cased method such as `__Construct` remains an ordinary method. [114f38dff3](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/114f38dff3df47bd98001b97a3fe734840e54ce3)
