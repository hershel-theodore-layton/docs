# 2023-01-17

## Highlights

- Several standard-library HHI output parameters now have concrete nullable types. APC success outputs are `inout ?bool`, while the error-code outputs of `msg_send()` and `msg_receive()` are `inout ?int`. Variables passed to these functions should use the corresponding nullable type and be checked after the call.
