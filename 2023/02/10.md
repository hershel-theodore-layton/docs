# 2023-02-10

## Highlights

- Sound Dynamic like-type pushing now handles generic lower bounds, allowing relationships such as `vec<~int> <: ~T` when `vec<int> <: T`. [4f90a4b70c](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/4f90a4b70c8496b8e86bca77c8a9137f5638bd27)
- HHVM now clears the implicit context at every VM entry boundary. Server-mode exceptions from warmup, shutdown, or presend hooks can no longer leak an implicit context into a subsequent request. [4e07ff06fc](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/4e07ff06fc22069240822ed14bc50c4f86bde68b)
