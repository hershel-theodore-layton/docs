## Highlights

- `hh --json --prefer-stdout` sends JSON results to standard output instead of mixing them with diagnostic output on standard error, simplifying scripted use. [69c5614feb](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/69c5614febe8f970c0294d0f360811f0c9767c17)
- Autocomplete now offers accessible type constants after `SomeClass::` and `this::` in type positions, including chained abstract and concrete accesses. [86d5445582](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/86d5445582bf7e783d5f0a8b19ed7b2fa8aace9b)
- When `HH_SERVER_PATH` is set, `hh_client` launches exactly that `hh_server` binary instead of relying on a sibling binary or `$PATH`. This provides deterministic pairing for custom builds, wrappers, and test environments. [edcc11a69a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/edcc11a69a0a2bf3d2f3410d7a84c7ee85be2da0)
