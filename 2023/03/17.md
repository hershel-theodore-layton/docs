- `hackfmt` now keeps expression-tree block braces next to their backticks, formatting them as ``Dsl`{ ... }` `` rather than separating the backtick and brace. [a665b6d472](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/a665b6d47235e4381ed00c29f64b5e73b36a21c5)
- The March 10 default requiring constant function and lambda default arguments has been reverted. Nonconstant defaults are accepted again unless the stricter options are explicitly enabled. [04b49bbdfe](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/04b49bbdfed97b4b612fc29ac53cba06612d5939)
