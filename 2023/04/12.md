## Future Changes

Set the `show` log level to at least 1 when the wrapper itself is needed for debugging. [9c5e8c2136](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9c5e8c21360c8a341e1bee5ed132bee32b081f9b)
