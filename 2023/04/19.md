# 2023-04-19

## Highlights

- XHP completion now closes the completed tag based on its `children` declaration: elements with `children empty` receive a self-closing tag, while elements that accept children receive matching open and close tags. [af22df5d2b](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/af22df5d2b451b3abc7af60443132690539818f1)
- The Sodium extension now provides Ed25519 scalar reduction, scalar addition and multiplication, and clamped or unclamped base-point scalar multiplication. Bundled Hack declarations follow on April 26. [9c58ed7053](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9c58ed70534f53f3515eb0ce678592f21479f256)
