# 2023-05-03

## Highlights

- HHVM gained experimental execution recording and replay support, including `hhvm_wrapper.php --record` and `--replay` modes for reproducing requests and native-call results. [ef45cfd538](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ef45cfd53823cfa1c0930e3b4d6f448223c429bf)
- When the typechecker is occupied while a client connects, `hh_client` now reports `hh_server is busy [connecting]` rather than appearing to hang without context. [c49bbae52f](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/c49bbae52f1185b28af7d21c035e202b596e4731)
