# 2023-05-11

## Highlights

- Sound Dynamic hover results no longer depend on whether another IDE request populated the TAST cache first. Cached ordinary and dynamic-assumption TASTs are now distinguished. [e2b26e3bbc](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e2b26e3bbcf323dbb3f25662662a5a0be9ae37b3)
- The IDE now offers an “inline variable” refactor for replacing a local variable with its initializer and removing the declaration when safe. [a37c556428](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/a37c556428739141b15abc70489a037ec0300ce2)

## Breaking Changes

- `<<__NoAutoDynamic>>` is no longer accepted on classes. Apply the opt-out to supported declarations such as individual methods or interfaces instead. [60560ff1fd](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/60560ff1fd4307e1fc81d5b45b93da58f6da9fa7)
