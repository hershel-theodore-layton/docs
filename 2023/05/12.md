# 2023-05-12

## Highlights

- Sound Dynamic checking now recognizes `<<__ReturnDisposable>>` through `~` and `supportdyn` wrappers, closing a hole where disposable-return rules could be bypassed. [5332fadec7](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5332fadec77d91f021c95b72ace4f8d38f7240be)

## Breaking Changes

- User-defined autoload maps and `autoload_set_paths()` support have been removed. Sandbox deployments use native Facts autoloading, while repo-authoritative deployments use `RepoAutoloadMap`. [6bb7530a87](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/6bb7530a8792196314c7b98ed03ce24586647cf3)
