# 2023-05-19

## Highlights

- `BuiltinEnum` APIs are now marked `__NoAutoDynamic`, removing unnecessary like-types from methods whose implementations are known to preserve their declared generic types. [b3df928ba8](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b3df928ba862faa17ea5b1a354657beb456c2ccc)
- Subtyping diagnostics involving a union with `dynamic` now prefer the incompatible non-dynamic member, producing more useful errors for ordinary expressions such as arithmetic. [7b5b19c496](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/7b5b19c4964b08f43e2a55a0d5161ec749f9e99e)
