- Impossible and redundant type-test lints now fire under implicit Sound Dynamic only when the ordinary and dynamic-assumption checks agree, suppressing migration-induced false positives. [923bd26f12](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/923bd26f129b683f939316d1f94a3c4ace842846)
- Tuple and shape subtyping now reports errors for multiple incompatible elements or fields instead of dropping every failure after the first conjunction. [b476fb30cf](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b476fb30cf1c6348e8dc842de3465e61d52f4535)
