# 2023-06-29

## Highlights

- The systemlib definitions of `HH\stdin()`, `HH\stdout()`, `HH\stderr()` and their nullable `try_*` variants now use `[]`, matching their existing HHI declarations; the HHI also gains documentation for script and web-request behavior. Moving the implementations from implicit `[defaults]` to `[]` narrows their capability requirement and is not a caller break. Obtaining a stream handle is pure, while performing I/O still requires the appropriate capability. [77b6d77442](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/77b6d7744276c66ba38408d33296f7d6ac9a7233)

## Future Changes

- The “Add local type hint” action is now offered only when the selection covers the assignment's left-hand variable or the complete assignment, avoiding unrelated suggestions when selecting part of the right-hand expression. [8c74b8200c](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/8c74b8200c581b2e2b29af2bf2a0ada44de2cae2)
