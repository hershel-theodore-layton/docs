# 2023-07-20

## Highlights

- `HH\parse_cookies()` parses a raw Cookie header into the same nested dict/vec shape used by `$_COOKIE`, without mutating request globals. [ad7a65180a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ad7a65180a03b9ac2118550d51383b204b291ddc)
- The splat operator may now contain `await` directly, enabling calls such as `f(...await $args)`. [b62d8f788f](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b62d8f788f6dd9153d2246d8400dce76af342dbd)
