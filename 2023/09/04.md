# 2023-09-04

## Highlights

- Enum-switch exhaustivity checking now recognizes pessimised enum types regardless of whether the enum bound appears before or after its like type in an intersection. This restores missing-case errors after awaiting an enum value and avoids treating intersections of unrelated enums as ordinary enum bounds. [d755a2e87b](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/d755a2e87b7fea6f08bfe889cf643aecae295a53)

## Future Changes

- Work begins on an opt-in strict-switch checker. Its first stage requires `default` for open-ended types and rejects a redundant `default` when the scrutinee has no possible values; broader literal and enum coverage is still being built. [ece08a97a1](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ece08a97a1c634a3a0a079a4fd417ec071f8a09a)
