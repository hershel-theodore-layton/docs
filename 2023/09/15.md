# 2023-09-15

## Highlights

- Nested blocks inside `finally` are now flattened correctly during elaboration, preventing a typechecker crash. [2df376a2e3](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/2df376a2e3187b184e64dff18e1e09942e53f01c)

[82316bc93d](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/82316bc93d241402cd9ee1d34896b6687b94e1cc)
