# 2023-09-20

## Highlights

- Hack's HHI hover text and manual now explicitly deprecate collection classes in favor of Hack arrays: prefer `vec` to `Vector`, `dict` to `Map`, `keyset` to `Set`, and tuples to `Pair`. [0fbfe6867e](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/0fbfe6867e45e0f04f4069f322fe4843ed12bb0b)
- Compiler-generated temporaries now have explicit block scope and are unset on both normal and exceptional exits. This also restores readonly checking for top-level `await` expressions inside `concurrent` blocks. [19d2d8a7af](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/19d2d8a7af5ff44ddaa0500ed7a188f960c6eec9)
