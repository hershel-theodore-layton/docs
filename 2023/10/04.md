# 2023-10-04

## Highlights

- HHVM can now serialize enum-class labels for memoization and use them as memo-table keys. The typechecker still rejects these parameters until its matching update on October 18. [c1e438cad1](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/c1e438cad10289dadc6fc6d46ef71083e85fb7a7)
- The debugger no longer resolves unrelated source and function breakpoints while loading units, functions, or classes, avoiding spurious breakpoint binding. [cc5e336780](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/cc5e336780941c726095c2429528acd34d8fac1e)

## Future Changes

- Experimental case types now preserve generic arguments when evaluating reified `is` checks and retain their nominal identity even when they contain a single variant. [580badd121](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/580badd12127f1695a664c81fe0dd91ca5787d85)
