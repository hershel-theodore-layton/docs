# 2023-10-11

## Breaking Changes

- The boolean `Eval.ClassnameNotices` setting has been replaced by `Eval.ClassnameNoticesSampleRate`. Use `0` to disable notices, `1` to emit every notice, or a larger value to sample approximately one in that many class-to-classname conversions.
