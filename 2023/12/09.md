# 2023-12-09

## Highlights

- `meth_caller(nameof C, 'method')` is now accepted as an alternative to `meth_caller(C::class, 'method')`. As before, the target must be a class rather than a trait. [b10117f022](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b10117f0227999080a7d1ec838e445f015f504a7)
