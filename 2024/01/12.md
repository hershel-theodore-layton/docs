# 2024-01-12

## Highlights

- XHP attribute checking no longer leaks refinements from its constructor-style pass into the second attribute-specification pass, eliminating spurious type holes and inaccurate TAST types. [524bddd78f](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/524bddd78f52d903a30860aea0df5d1184de5d5a)

## Breaking Changes

- Magic method names are now recognized with their exact Hack casing. A differently cased name is treated as an ordinary method and no longer receives magic-method-specific validation. [64f425c7df](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/64f425c7df912491f0e66394e12ad8d893888ccf)
