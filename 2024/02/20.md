# 2024-02-20

## Highlights

- The IDE now offers both a refactoring and an error quick-fix to prepend `await` to an `Awaitable`. When required, the complete action also makes the enclosing function, method, or lambda `async` and changes its return annotation to `Awaitable<...>`.
