# 2024-02-23

## Highlights

- Expressions with built-in `vec` and `dict` types no longer acquire unnecessary expression-dependent wrappers. Their inferred unions and error messages now show ordinary types such as `vec<C>` instead of `<expr#N> as vec<C>`.
