# 2024-03-27

## Highlights

- The new `unserialize_slice(string, int, int, darray = dict[])[defaults]: mixed` builtin deserializes a byte range without first allocating a substring. Invalid offsets or non-positive lengths throw `InvalidArgumentException`. [c3519b4365](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/c3519b436542b04885932090384c86c2e5470e31)
- `debugger_dump()` now writes exclusively through debugger stdout hooks, so debug-display output reaches the debugger without also entering the application's ordinary output buffer. [e5794da899](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e5794da8996e56627a36edb8b16044c9265d716b)
