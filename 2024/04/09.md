# 2024-04-09

## Highlights

- `Str\replace_every_nonrecursive()` is now declared with the empty capability list `[]`. Its previous omitted list meant `[defaults]`; narrowing the callee requirement to `[]` is non-breaking and lets callers with any capability superset—including pure callers—use it.
