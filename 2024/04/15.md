# 2024-04-15

## Breaking Changes

- `where` clauses may no longer project a type constant through a generic or abstract receiver, such as `where U = T::TC`. Express the relationship with a refinement such as `T as Base with { type TC = U }`. Context-polymorphic coeffect projections such as `[$a::Ctx]` and `[T::Ctx]` are explicitly exempt and remain supported.
