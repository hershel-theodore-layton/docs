# 2024-04-25

## Highlights

- Repo-authoritative optimization now models the concrete values written to memoization caches, avoiding incorrect bottom or return types for forwarded late-static-binding calls such as memoized methods inherited from traits. [9056a5bf64](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9056a5bf64df15b923d93084d8f0920df2cb810c)
- Top-level autocomplete backed by Glean now matches a declaration's autocomplete sort text as well as its name, allowing appropriately ranked declarations to appear for additional prefixes. [891e663ebc](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/891e663ebc22acdf7da68a16e128f7d367a166c1)

## Breaking Changes

- Hack's text diagnostic formats now encode severity. In particular, `--error-format plain` prefixes diagnostics with `ERROR:` or `WARN:`, and the raw, contextual, and highlighted renderers distinguish warnings too. Update scripts that parse the previous plain-text layout. [9bbf0bd8bd](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9bbf0bd8bdb2421b158512c750139fd6dff70a26) [4f788c05ab](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/4f788c05ab4d83fa10620c8ba924436c93189480) [ac2791f216](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ac2791f216b28a11eed364cd9c4ed24f388c6069) [3059d50fb7](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/3059d50fb75990ead7dc860b7d40372e991a17ee)

## Future Changes

- A disabled-by-default `hack_warnings` project option introduces non-fatal Hack warnings. The first warns when `?X` and `?Y` are compared even though the non-null primitive types `X` and `Y` are disjoint. [a5295f752a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/a5295f752a6c233d296e8f3a02df6072237e1d68) [f5f47a9cc6](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/f5f47a9cc6bfcf263c0b64a2335bb74f2ec0f114)
- Repo-authoritative optimization now handles enum members and unresolved object members in experimental case-type unions without misclassifying or asserting on the union. [ba2eace20a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ba2eace20a66dc98d3367ad464605fb760206390)
