# 2024-05-01

## Highlights

- “Add method stub” is now available when the cursor is on whitespace between class-body declarations, and inserts the selected inherited method at that position. [60ebc78364](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/60ebc783646ca04c85fd10940ee8b85c4a9ec306)
- Typechecking cyclic generic upper bounds no longer recurses indefinitely while resolving type-member constraints. [bcdba1ecd8](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/bcdba1ecd85b23177b533400afe66b14bb53b8a6)

## Future Changes

- The experimental strict switch checker is now selected project-wide by the `strict_switch` configuration. The per-switch `<<__StrictSwitch>>` attribute and its unstable-feature opt-in no longer select the implementation. [e67c9a80f0](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e67c9a80f0e5db2f99a87842d4346748b3e3da4e)
