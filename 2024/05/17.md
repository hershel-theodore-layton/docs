# 2024-05-17

## Highlights

- Hack now reports top-level `break` or `continue` inside a `finally` block during typechecking, matching the fatal that HackC already emitted. [cba8993c7c](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/cba8993c7cb91b3cd4a068e61650edc4e03ec1d9)

## Breaking Changes

- The erased `UNSAFE_CAST` pseudo-function may no longer be captured as a first-class function pointer with `UNSAFE_CAST<>`. Direct `HH\FIXME\UNSAFE_CAST(...)` migration calls remain available. [080efb3351](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/080efb33510fee5088d21063d948457e46e460e1)
