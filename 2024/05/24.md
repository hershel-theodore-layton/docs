# 2024-05-24

## Highlights

- The `hack_warnings` configuration now accepts `true`/`all`, `false`/`none`, or a comma-separated allowlist of warning codes such as `12005,12006`. Projects can enable a targeted warning migration without taking every warning at once. [dbacc3fc0c](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/dbacc3fc0c60d1f8444463c8b3c5bb09eb2bd430)

## Future Changes

- Under implicit pessimisation, `is this` and `as this` now refine to `~this` where an exact `this` result would be unsound. [2e1a710c6b](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/2e1a710c6b82f60576b771e348245d5818b03c2f)
- Enum refinements nested inside nullable and tuple-like types now preserve the enum's stringish or intish weakening instead of unsoundly producing an exact enum type. [9235b6c767](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9235b6c767a7e062ead992a6a8d0d606eccc4951)
