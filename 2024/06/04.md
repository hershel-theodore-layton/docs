# 2024-06-04

## Breaking Changes

- The pure `HH\darray()` and `HH\varray()` conversion functions are now deprecated. Use `dict()` and `vec()` respectively. [4330501899](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/4330501899a288085fd502da1cb9eaad41a4d5b9)
