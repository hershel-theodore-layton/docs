# 2024-08-02

## Highlights

- The implicit-context primitives for reading, testing, and creating keyed contexts have narrowed their capability requirement from `[zoned]` to `[leak_safe]`, reducing what callers must provide and allowing memo-agnostic contexts to run in leak-safe code. [b31c1e86ac](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b31c1e86accddac17cf55a0679b7d34f630979c2)
- Extended type-error explanations now track tuple projections during destructuring and property-access flows explicitly. Their formatter also prints a source position before its snippet, making longer derivations easier to follow. [a69db169be](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/a69db169be1969e31056b3a3f3f0b33338566dc7) [04885b2ab4](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/04885b2ab47aee6ce4924c8a30123654e60a4ec6) [6c0058f649](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/6c0058f649762a776d7be535dccb02b8573c0acf)

## Breaking Changes

- Custom `HH\ImplicitContext` subclasses must now define `CRun` with at least the `[leak_safe]` requirement: the abstract context constant gained an `as [leak_safe]` bound, so a smaller requirement such as `[]` no longer satisfies it. `[leak_safe]`, `[zoned]`, and `[defaults]` definitions remain valid. [b31c1e86ac](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b31c1e86accddac17cf55a0679b7d34f630979c2)
