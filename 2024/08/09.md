- The new `.hhconfig` option `warnings_default_all` chooses whether bare `hh` behaves like `hh -Wall` or `hh -Wnone`, including while errors are streamed. Its default is false. [d22e238d66](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/d22e238d668209d831f567ef9f6cc8001ef0a718)
- Constraint-based refinement now handles `~nonnull` tested against `null` correctly by moving `dynamic` outside only on the false side of a type switch. [90eff9efc1](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/90eff9efc1511975b6f41d5b892cde56d929d97b)
