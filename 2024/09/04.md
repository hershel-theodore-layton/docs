# 2024-09-04

## Breaking Changes

- Hack warning generation is enabled by default, including in development installations that do not supply a local rollout configuration. The local `hh.conf` setting `hack_warnings` controls generation; warning display remains separately controlled by `warnings_default_all` and the CLI warning flags.
