# 2024-09-22

## Breaking Changes

- Deprecated implicit-context states and memoization options have been removed. The `NULL`, `SOFT_SET`, and `SOFT_INACCESSIBLE` state members and the `SoftMakeICInaccessible` and `Uncategorized` memoization options are no longer available; use the remaining `VALUE` / `INACCESSIBLE` states and `KeyedByIC` / `MakeICInaccessible` options.
