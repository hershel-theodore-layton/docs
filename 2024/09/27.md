# 2024-09-27

## Highlights

- Qualified `nameof` expressions such as `nameof N\C` are now accepted as constant expressions by the typechecker, including in enum initializers. [7761a6b906](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/7761a6b906f8def31834d9cb49b8068122b001b0)
- When initializing from a clean public revision, Hack records its warning hashes and discards those baseline warnings, reducing unrelated warning noise after moving from the saved-state revision to a merge base. [9d511a4f90](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9d511a4f906998d7af4af1b25c13bb827a482bf2)
