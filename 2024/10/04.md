# 2024-10-04

## Highlights

- Warning filtering now tolerates failures from its Mercurial queries instead of allowing an external VCS error to abort the typecheck. [9b5597b79f](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9b5597b79fdd58d0baca9e847766066fab6bdff3)
- At positive typing-log levels, `hh_show()` output now includes numbered type variables such as `#1`, which makes unresolved relationships easier to follow. [ca6767e0a1](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ca6767e0a137cd2f55ea64c28c9b13162005412e)

## Future Changes

- Packages v2 `include_paths` now use repository-rooted paths beginning with `//`; directory entries end in `/`, exact-file entries do not, and `./` or `../` segments are rejected. [9bcaba8f44](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9bcaba8f4486c2b08d7487d34af317f5ab4110bc)
- The experimental type-splat syntax can now appear at the end of tuple hints, for example `(float, ...T)`. A tuple may have only one variadic or splat tail, and a splat cannot follow an optional element. [03ed3cc3d6](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/03ed3cc3d6ecc80052b329e037c3c4ba8032f63a)
