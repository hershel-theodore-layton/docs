# 2024-10-30

## Highlights

- HHVM now populates `$_SERVER['REQUEST_TIME_MONOTONIC_NS']` from the monotonic clock alongside the existing realtime request timestamps, enabling elapsed-time measurements that are not affected by wall-clock adjustments. [5226b11299](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5226b112990a942660672a4d66262e074199f4d7)

## Future Changes

- HHVM can now execute the Unstable shape-destructuring syntax, including nested list/shape targets, local and indexed destinations, and string, `nameof`, or class-constant shape keys. [54a4bac107](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/54a4bac107d78a582f2b6dfeeb0aa90b56756d71)
