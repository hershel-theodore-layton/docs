# 2024-11-22

## Highlights

- In `--everything-sdt` mode, `<<__NoSupportDynamicType>>` suppresses the implicit `<<__SupportDynamicType>>` on a declaration while retaining signature pessimisation, `supportdyn` wrapping, and implicit `supportdyn<mixed>` bounds. [1e83a3bfa7](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/1e83a3bfa7dd95bd2f03155c36959f46a6cf313d)

## Future Changes

- Experimental tuple splats now participate in tuple subtyping: when comparing `(T1, T2, ...T)` with another tuple, Hack checks the fixed prefix and compares `T` with the remaining tuple tail. [02b3e770ab](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/02b3e770abe1c3db2673d275760befb26eaf4ebe)
- Recursive case types must now be contractive. Direct or nullable self aliases such as `case type A = A` and `case type A = ?A` are rejected, while recursion underneath a constructor such as `case type A = vec<A>` remains supported. [5748214e09](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5748214e093cc6f3ce0b469c80f5d2295cdaf2df)
