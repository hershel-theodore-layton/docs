# 2024-12-11

## Highlights

- A top-level generic function pointer now retains a universally quantified function type. A reference such as `identity<>` can therefore instantiate independently as `(function(int): int)` and `(function(string): string)` at different uses, while respecting its generic bounds and where-constraints.
