# 2024-12-12

## Highlights

- Initial warning suppression is now applied before errors are streamed, preventing baseline warnings from being sent to the client before the end-of-check filter can remove them. [e93229f61e](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e93229f61e2a41aeec4ff12ed9eef6d5403f66dd)
- `HH\TypeStructureKind` now exposes `OF_CLASS_PTR` and `OF_CLASS_OR_CLASSNAME`, matching the `kind` values returned by `type_structure()` and `ReflectionTypeConstant::getTypeStructure()` for `class<T>` and transitional class-or-classname types. [14a113c6d0](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/14a113c6d091b8d1b9812e39a4a1d524c27a4b9e)
