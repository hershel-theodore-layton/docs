# 2025-01-31

## Breaking Changes

- The `report_pos_from_reason` typechecker option has been removed. Diagnostics no longer append `[FROM REASON INFO]`, and positions derived from type reasons no longer receive special treatment that prevented `HH_FIXME` or `HH_IGNORE_ERROR` suppression.
