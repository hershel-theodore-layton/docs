# 2025-02-13

## Future Changes

- Behind `safe_abstract=true`, Hack gained a prototype for preventing unsafe construction and static calls through class names that may denote abstract classes. It introduces `concreteclassname<T>` and `<<__NeedsConcrete>>`; a needs-concrete method may only be called through a class known to be concrete, treats `static` as concrete within its body, and may not override a method that lacks the stronger requirement.
