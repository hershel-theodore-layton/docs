# 2025-03-11

## Highlights

- The missing-method-visibility diagnostic now lists `internal` as a valid choice and offers a quick-fix that inserts it. [c2ab98a8ad](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/c2ab98a8adf81eaf2afdde93dd9d214ba24745c6)

## Breaking Changes

- Runtime-enforceable hint validation now resets its “inside reified argument” state between sibling generic arguments. Invalid `is` hints containing erased arguments after a reified argument are no longer accidentally accepted. [28716a94fb](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/28716a94fbb8d7c0bf6612df5cac982434dad59e)

## Future Changes

- Safe-abstract typing replaces the special-purpose `concreteclassname<T>` with the general covariant wrapper `concrete<T>`. This supports both `concrete<classname<T>>` and, with class-pointer typing enabled, `concrete<class<T>>`; the feature remains controlled by `safe_abstract`, which defaults to `false`. [f3468eb107](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/f3468eb1070649e9ac2a2753f673c3c9128ad974)
- Class-pointer intersection simplification now reduces `class<U> & class<V>` to `class<U & V>` and, when `class<T>` is configured as a subtype of `classname<T>`, reduces `class<U> & classname<V>` to `classname<U & V>`. This gives equality refinement a denotable result instead of retaining an opaque intersection. [def2bf2676](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/def2bf267678ccf070d9fbf495692799eaf7ccb4)
