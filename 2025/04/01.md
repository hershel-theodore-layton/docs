# 2025-04-01

## Highlights

- Redundant or impossible `is` and `as` checks are now detected after stripping the `dynamic` part of most like types, revealing more always-true and always-false checks without producing false positives for `~null`. [cb98bf2069](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/cb98bf2069d5b0cacad67aeb0efbefbc04bd429e)

## Future Changes

- Packages v2 `include_paths` entries in `PACKAGES.toml` must now end in `/`. This prevents a path such as `flib/intern` from also matching `flib/internal_prod`. [5f584f16cb](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5f584f16cbbeeac75d44a58d384dbdc54460a0dc)
