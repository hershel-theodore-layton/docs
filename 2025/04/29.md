# 2025-04-29

## Highlights

- `<<__Memoize(#MakeICInaccessible)>>` is now accepted on functions requiring zoned or `newctx` capabilities. The special parser ban was removed without changing the function's declared context requirement. [1a7cebca38](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/1a7cebca38e258908bc4613ae91bd7145dc50797)

## Future Changes

[6bee4c575e](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/6bee4c575e9ec561dfb2d6efb4b0976a1e9fa7fd)
