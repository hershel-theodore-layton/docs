# 2025-05-12

## Breaking Changes

- `HH\TypeStructure<T>` now models `generic_types`, `root_name`, and `access_list` as optional fields. Use `??`, `Shapes::idx()`, or a key-presence check rather than assuming the fields exist. [0e0fb9a62e](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/0e0fb9a62e305c789e1172869d0880c5ccf906ff)

## Future Changes

- The unstable `protected internal` visibility modifier is now accepted, as is `internal protected`. Its access and override rules combine subclass visibility with module-internal visibility. [9a4623425f](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9a4623425fba05012e79940e0fd5561aa07b7a75)
- HHVM's compiler now also rejects memoization attributes on unstable type-splat functions, completing the checker-side restriction added on May 9. [34727f0d85](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/34727f0d8588a0c251d77d12ae083e90d459ab5a)
