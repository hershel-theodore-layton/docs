# 2025-05-23

## Highlights

- Repo-authoritative builds can use repeatable `--static-pattern` glob options to include only selected files in `file.cache`; this mode cannot be combined with `--cdir` or `--cfile`. [51c53a7882](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/51c53a7882311765c06f74f9da8a90fc152b45ff) [bd70a8f60a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/bd70a8f60ace32fc3eccafeac50fe72e4d13068a)
- HSL adds `HH\Lib\Async\LowPri<T>` for starting non-critical async work at low priority and promoting it later. `run()` has callee requirements `[ctx $action, write_props]`, and `prioritize()` requires `[write_props]`, so their callers must provide at least those capabilities. [b90e9d07ac](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b90e9d07acd026be0aed76c1dea57fcc3159e011)
- The HHI for `PriorityBridgeWaitHandle::prioritize()` now explicitly uses `[]` instead of omitted brackets. This weakens its callee requirement from implicit `[defaults]` to no capabilities and is therefore non-breaking for callers. [b90e9d07ac](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b90e9d07acd026be0aed76c1dea57fcc3159e011)
