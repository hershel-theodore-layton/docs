# 2025-06-14

## Highlights

- APC and HHVM-internal serialization now preserve class pointers as lazy-class `l:` values instead of turning them into strings, including when nested in `Pair`, `Map`, or `dict` values. Ordinary user-facing `serialize()` remains string-based by default. [159314f561](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/159314f561676cc405c1eecd5650aacfe8b6cc1e)
