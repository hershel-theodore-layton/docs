# 2025-06-30

## Future Changes

- When the experimental EdenFS-backed file watcher cannot initialize, `hh_server` now falls back to the next enabled notifier—normally Watchman—instead of failing outright. [6d4cfd4936](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/6d4cfd4936f5a183a8cf9971c4125d053ceae6a8)
- Nested type-parameter declarations used for experimental higher-kinded types, such as `class Foo<TC<TX>> {}`, are no longer part of Hack's grammar. The `--enable-higher-kinded-types` single-file checker flag and its internal option have also been removed; projects must express abstractions with supported ordinary generics. [b0f3ed5c9c](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b0f3ed5c9cdb2cad3f34d55e757074f86fee1f75) [60adf9a9fb](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/60adf9a9fb21e6d4f7e3b394bcd9361c64fd34aa)
