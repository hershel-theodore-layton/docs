# 2025-07-15

## Highlights

- Implicit string-to-class conversion notices now identify whether the conversion came from an expression, a static-method call, `type_structure()`, or `HH\dynamic_class_meth()`, making class-pointer migrations easier to triage. [d6f352bc64](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/d6f352bc64c646333495d499068126152142878e)
- The compact-protocol readers now accept an optional trailing `int $version = 2` at runtime, and the public HHI exposes it on `thrift_protocol_read_compact_struct_from_string()`. Its omitted capability list remains implicit `[defaults]`. [c292d2c41e](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/c292d2c41ec66f491bf3f7cdf78494df68d20814)

## Future Changes

- Staged type-splat checking now accepts an ordinary literal lambda where a concrete expanded function signature is expected. [16001a1770](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/16001a1770004072b91ef2fec965882833b94ef6)
- Polymorphic lambdas and polymorphic function hints have moved to OngoingRelease, but remain a staged feature. [edf5bb9a63](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/edf5bb9a63b4108ebc4adf7557b92b9fa0b8bbc7)
- Expression trees add staged null-safe property and method access, exposed to DSL visitors through `visitPropertyAccessNullSafe` and `visitInstanceMethodNullSafe`. [1dfaf9d703](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/1dfaf9d7031c1e78375586b760b556bab43d669d)
