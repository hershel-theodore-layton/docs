# 2025-08-04

## Highlights

- Arity diagnostics now point at the call subexpression whose argument count is wrong, rather than a broader enclosing expression. [ab8c323b64](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ab8c323b645532a804f65729f14f745f5c34048e)
- Incremental typechecking now records more of the declaration dependencies consulted while checking a file. This closes cases where an incremental check could miss an error found by a clean check, at the possible cost of somewhat larger recheck fanout. [290788b789](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/290788b78993b7cc5f5b72516212824f0f6dc0f6)
