# 2025-08-11

## Highlights

- `hh_client check --single` now processes batches of ten or more files in parallel. The new `--multi FILE` form reads one path per line, making large targeted checks easier to script. [8c8f596073](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/8c8f596073530d6b604de41ce7f8cadc3cef13eb) [5ca15ac9d8](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5ca15ac9d8b3db9cc26e79834f6246791f98230b)
- The dead-`HH_FIXME` and dead-unsafe-cast cleanup modes no longer require restarting `hh_server` with `--no-load`. [e7f422bb80](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e7f422bb80e469f71b0fe34bf69a671766122836)

## Future Changes
