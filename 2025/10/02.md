# 2025-10-02

## Highlights

- Typing[4104] and Typing[4105] now say "too few positional arguments" and "too many positional arguments." Named-argument arity has separate diagnostics, so the revised wording identifies the part of a call that is actually wrong. [9a285f64ab](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9a285f64abe33396737e08094ff1ab038ba3b19b)

## Future Changes

- Unstable named parameters now participate in function-type subtyping and override arity checks. A subtype must accept every named parameter promised by its supertype and may not make an optional named parameter required. [a354304453](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/a354304453c7765ba53d4dfde417f00fa9196c6e)
