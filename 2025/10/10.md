# 2025-10-10

## Security

- Servers can set `Server.AllowExec=false` to disable process-launching APIs. HHVM then throws for `shell_exec()`, `exec()`, `passthru()`, `system()`, `proc_open()`, `pcntl_exec()`, and HSL's `OS\fork_and_execve()`; the default remains `true`. [5a71f2f16a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5a71f2f16a1324091e007718426f2d4416eed62f)

## Future Changes

- The staged null-safe pipe operator `|?>` evaluates its right-hand side only when the left-hand side is non-null. Within the right-hand side `$$` is narrowed to non-null, and the whole expression remains nullable. [1ba856e4ee](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/1ba856e4ee585eca676db56ab7e0483e0ef85f41)
