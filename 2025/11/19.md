# 2025-11-19

## Highlights

- HHBBC no longer performs stack-rewinding or stack-shape-changing reductions while speculating through a block, fixing a path that could incorrectly optimize bytecode. [58b4eb518a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/58b4eb518ab6a5577db2dd4299d68b1b2af5c71a)

## Breaking Changes

- The runtime functions `apache_request_headers()` and its alias `getallheaders()` have been removed. Their declarations had already been removed from the standard-library HHI files. [be51b361e8](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/be51b361e85e3d9a810e476293c2d97da485bfa0)

## Future Changes

- `.hhconfig` gains `check_bool_for_condition`: `0` keeps existing truthiness behavior, `1` emits Warn[12028], and `2` emits Typing[4504] unless conditions are subtypes of `~bool`. The opt-in check covers control-flow conditions, `!`, `&&`, `||`, and ternaries. [f2102bc763](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/f2102bc763d0c7d0a11f3924a0f21039e5cae929)
