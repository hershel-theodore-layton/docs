# 2025-12-02

## Highlights

- The Thrift extension now provides `thrift_protocol_write_binary_struct_to_string()` and `thrift_protocol_read_binary_struct_from_string()`. They serialize Binary-protocol structs directly to and from strings, avoiding repeated calls into Hack through `TMemoryBuffer`; both omit explicit capability brackets and therefore require `[defaults]`.
