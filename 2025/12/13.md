# 2025-12-13

## Highlights

- `AsyncMysqlQueryResult` now exposes `mysqlInfo(): ?string`, `rowsMatched(): ?int`, and `warningsCount(): int`. These distinguish rows matched from rows actually changed by an update and expose server warning metadata; their omitted coeffect brackets mean `[defaults]`, so they are not callable from a pure `[]` context.
