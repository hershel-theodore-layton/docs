## Highlights

- The `thrift_protocol_write_binary_struct_to_string()` and `thrift_protocol_read_binary_struct_from_string()` built-ins first added on December 2 have relanded after the December 18 rollback. They serialize and deserialize binary-protocol Thrift structs without constructing a transport object. Both signatures omit coeffect brackets and therefore require `[defaults]`.
