# 2026-01-29

## Highlights

- The `HH\autoload_*_to_path()`, `HH\autoload_path_to_*()`, and `HH\autoload_is_native()` declarations now explicitly require `[]` instead of their former implicit `[defaults]`. This narrows their caller requirements, allowing these deterministic autoload lookups from pure contexts; it is a capability relaxation, not a breaking change. [5c73ddb336](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5c73ddb33665ee39d8a303d0778d19ca2d75262c)
- Runtime `is` checks against a reified shape or tuple generic now first verify that the value has the matching type-structure kind, preventing a segfault when the actual value has a different kind. [9f0e9f6d43](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/9f0e9f6d43d209e66f03498b141d4f5256c51df5)
