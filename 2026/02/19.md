# 2026-02-19

## Highlights

- `openssl_pkcs7_read(string $data): vec<string>` reads PEM-encoded signed or signed-and-enveloped PKCS#7 data and returns its certificates as PEM strings, throwing when the payload cannot be parsed. Its omitted coeffect brackets mean callers must provide at least `[defaults]`. [4cd3b1fce0](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/4cd3b1fce018fe7ee2d725c9af9e9b2697238c2e)

## Future Changes

- The named-parameter interpreter now supports omitted optional named parameters by validating the supplied names and filling absent slots with their default values. Variadic packing also preserves the preceding named arguments, while HHBBC continues to avoid unsafe folding of these calls.
