# 2026-02-26

## Breaking Changes

- Type-constant propagation now skips `mixed` and `supportdyn<mixed>` upper bounds instead of expanding `mixed::T` into `Tany`. This closes a soundness hole in `where`-clause inference and can reveal errors that were previously hidden after related type variables collapsed to `Tany`.
