# 2026-03-30

## Highlights

- HackLint 5644's quick fix now produces the correct boolean result for always-true and always-false boolean expressions: `expr || true` becomes `true`, and `expr && false` becomes `false`, rather than incorrectly dropping the literal and keeping `expr`. [2961c64c7b](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/2961c64c7b48a3806bcc55d31ec46aa1fb184a41)
- Warn[12026] for calling `new static()` through an uninstantiable class now recommends `<<__NeedsConcrete>>` on the containing method and offers a quick fix to add it. [4606faa9c0](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/4606faa9c0e2201ec8858e0617de5f73eeb28e9b)

## Future Changes

- The pre-stable typed-local declaration syntax was removed from the parser, HackC, typechecker, IDE actions, and experimental-feature registry. Projects experimenting with `let $x: T` must return to ordinary local assignments. [599dea0aa0](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/599dea0aa083c26edfe33667880f663e63b0fe76) [68b41e1558](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/68b41e15582565fed8bdb250034da62495614e8a)
- With `strict_consistent_construct=true`, Warn[12037] now reports `abstract final` classes marked `<<__ConsistentConstruct>>`. This replaces the earlier ban on abstract classes extending non-abstract consistent-construction classes; ordinary abstract subclasses are allowed again. [ae16b6ede2](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ae16b6ede279c912ed97f9c9ce05af0cc9ddbf5f)
