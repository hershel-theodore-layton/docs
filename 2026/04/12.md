# 2026-04-12

## Future Changes

- Under the preview readonly feature, `HH\Readonly\as_mut()` checking now recursively applies the same value-type rules as `HH\Readonly\is_value_type()`. Nested structures containing non-value data, such as a shape containing a closure, are rejected statically instead of passing Hack and failing at runtime.
