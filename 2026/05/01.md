# 2026-05-01

## Highlights

- Redundant `__PackageOverride` analysis can now verify dependency-graph candidates against their typed syntax, excluding references such as `nameof`, `::class`, and attributes that do not affect the production build. This makes package-override linting less prone to conservative false positives. [f41333f091](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/f41333f0914899cbd8734b79841c03c4c5f0b722)

## Future Changes

- Preview `HH\Runtime\reveal()` is now explicitly pure (`[]`). This narrows its caller requirement from omitted brackets—which mean `[defaults]`—to no capabilities, so it can be called from pure code; `representable_as` itself remains in Preview. [6cbf331237](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/6cbf33123717b449991dbc649291440dcb7af894)
