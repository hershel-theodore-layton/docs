# 2026-05-05

## Breaking Changes

- `ReflectionMethod::invoke()` and `invokeArgs()` are now deprecated in Hack declarations. Migrate reflective call sites to `HH_FIXME::reflectionMethodInvokeForCallsite()` and `HH_FIXME::reflectionMethodInvokeArgsForCallsite()` while removing avoidable reflection. [5d4b0335e7](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/5d4b0335e701246567c8555fa383ebd1a501d73f)

## Future Changes

- Preview expression trees can now desugar `vec[...]`, `dict[...]`, and `keyset[...]` literals through `visitVec`, `visitDict`, and `visitKeyset`. The support is gated by `expression_tree_hack_arrays`. [b8379a88ac](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/b8379a88ace3b9fb31360670fd4f57bd18e33296)
- The staged named-parameter runtime now has dedicated function entries for optional named parameters and can initialize their defaults consistently in the interpreter and JIT, including coeffect checks after initialization. Named parameters are not yet Released.
