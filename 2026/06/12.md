# 2026-06-12

## Highlights

- A class may combine `require this as C` with `require extends C` or `require class C` for the same target without HHVM incorrectly reporting conflicting trait requirements. Combining `require class` with `require extends` still reports a conflict. [358d0b7f35](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/358d0b7f350e826b1ad027df9d2fa6baa7368e01)

- HHVM avoids a race in the file-await destructor caused by reading its event-base-owned file-descriptor map from another thread; this fixes crashes observed on ARM. [93473f0997](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/93473f09971b2f37246dc3fc29733ab60347aa22)
