# 2026-06-25

## Highlights

- Single-file and IDE typechecking now honor file-level `__EnableUnstableFeatures` attributes consistently with a full check, avoiding spurious feature-gating errors in opted-in files. [6700457943](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/670045794396f85f48af4c0567b4e45e2c939fe7)

## Breaking Changes

- Cross-package references to non-class-like type aliases are now always checked in runtime-enforced positions, including parameter, return, and property hints and `as` constraints. The `package_allow_enforceable_type_alias_violations` escape hatch has been removed. [65c848a477](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/65c848a477501a57a6e05a220ecf0214adee91a4)
