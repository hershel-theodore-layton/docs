# 2026-06-26

## Highlights

- `is vec_or_dict<_>` now participates in predicate-based refinement, preserving useful information about the vec or dict branches and their inferred key types. [e09ba51794](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e09ba51794a9db0fb9c957128bbd852fdfc71e60)

## Breaking Changes

- The typechecker always rejects `(bool)` casts; the `disallow_bool_cast` project option has been removed. [7c19298944](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/7c19298944f2c8f10cac4c382551c5bac9a1d6e4)

- Function pointers are no longer permitted in constant expressions or property initializers. The `disallow_func_ptrs_in_constants` and `Hack.Lang.DisallowFuncPtrsInConstants` options have been removed. [167cdef425](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/167cdef425ef5df5a0b52f910c1b57916526794a)

- The `@` error-suppression operator is unconditionally rejected by the Hack typechecker; the `disallow_silence` option has been removed. [ead6d41ac8](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/ead6d41ac8e217d57bb9bc062684d26b34f1ba99)

- The typechecker always reports redundant generic parameters, and the `check_redundant_generics` option has been removed. [0f73b73373](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/0f73b73373f465306cb3f62ea1a1b9920db155e5)
