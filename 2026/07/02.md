# 2026-07-02

## Highlights

- Open-source HHVM no longer collects its low-level per-request JIT and interpreter timing measurements. On platforms where reading current-thread CPU time requires `clock_gettime()`, collecting these measurements consumed about 37% of CPU time during JIT warmup; removing them avoids that overhead.
