# 2026-07-15

## Highlights

- HHBBC preserves unused compound arithmetic operations that can throw, including division or modulo by zero and non-numeric operands. A fractional modulo divisor that truncates to zero is also handled safely. [057bffc3ee](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/057bffc3ee8969809b7c1d1264e83ef46f9b76ef)

## Breaking Changes

- Numeric literals containing underscore separators, such as `1_000`, are no longer accepted. Remove separators from integer and floating-point literals. [e05fa0d5ca](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e05fa0d5ca847e6656a8f0d127a63260c79806bc)
