# 2026-08-14

## Highlights

- The JIT no longer assumes that an unloaded user type alias denotes an object, preventing incorrect handling of shape or dict values returned synchronously, through `await`, or through `inout` parameters. [e02dc92e31](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/e02dc92e310ae00834b7c83613a1d689a4d1884a)

## Future Changes

- Typed open-shape subtyping now compares the types of unknown fields, rejecting incompatible tails such as `shape(string...)` where `shape(int...)` is required. [8bbd0ed223](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/8bbd0ed22397bb86caec9b564bc09945830a01a6)

- Expression-tree subscripting has advanced from `Preview` to `OngoingRelease` in the experimental-feature rollout. [72a8b4317f](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/72a8b4317f30d3d3d715891d2df2cb7257890a3e)
