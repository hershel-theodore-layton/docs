## Highlights

- Setting `Eval.AllowManualGC=false` makes `gc_collect_cycles()` skip collection. Manual collection remains enabled by default. [bb180afe2e](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/bb180afe2e568c782c05cedfed94e1e87a07d635)
