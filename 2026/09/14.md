# 2026-09-14

## Highlights

- `serialize()` accepts an options dictionary, including `zstd=true`; `HH\serialize_with_options()` and `unserialize()` also support streaming zstd compression or decompression. This avoids materializing the full uncompressed serialized string at once. [35e214ff7a](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/35e214ff7aadd1a23832ab009cdff88c79fce064)

- `HH\ImplicitContext\PreparedContext::runBatch()` and `runBatchAsync()` no longer fail with a coeffect violation before running callbacks with `[]` or `[zoned_shallow]` contexts. Their shared preparation helper now declares `[leak_safe]`. [1d894fd5cc](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/1d894fd5cce291c54975382e5f4818864c8dcc92)

## Breaking Changes

- Files assigned to implicit packages by their paths may no longer use `__PackageOverride` to escape that membership. [51094a61e4](https://github.com/hershel-theodore-layton/hhvm/commit/51094a61e4b1fd55c7b199594b4eedcaa5fd31a1)
