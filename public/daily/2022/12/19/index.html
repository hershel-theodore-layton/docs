Breaking Changes

  • SplPriorityQueue now takes separate value and priority type parameters: SplPriorityQueue<TValue, TPriority>. Its bundled HHI also gives concrete types to compare(), insert(), extract(), top(), and the iterator methods. Existing one-argument annotations must add the priority type. b55314d75a
  • Constant-expression validation now recursively rejects static::* and parent::class inside collection and shape literals used for constants or property initializers. These accesses were already rejected at the top level; nesting them no longer bypasses the check. 3edc239bad
