Highlights

  • Sound-dynamic typing of Shapes::idx(), Shapes::at(), and Shapes::keyExists() now follows their transformed HHI declarations. Shape and Pair operations are no longer automatically pessimised, and Hack-array element bounds rely on supportdyn pushing instead. Projects should see more precise element types and errors that consistently enforce SDT arguments. b0c6a3527b 1c3dd600e1 e707d1fd3b 3ffec61090
  • <<__NoAutoDynamic>> is now honored when a trait is parsed directly; annotated traits are no longer incorrectly treated as supporting dynamic under implicit pessimization. d48859c686
  • Enum classes now participate in implicit sound-dynamic pessimization like ordinary classes. 53a512f6d9
