Highlights

  • Invalid PACKAGES.toml relationships now produce parsing error 1009. The checker reports references to undefined packages and module globs assigned to more than one package. fbc8e0d7bb
  • Implicit pessimization of an async function returning an enforced enum now applies the enum's base-type intersection inside Awaitable<...>, rather than intersecting the awaitable itself down to nothing. 95dae7a057
  • Enum classes may now carry the internal SDT marker used by implicit sound-dynamic mode, avoiding recursive subtype failures for pessimised enum classes. 4ff56661ca
