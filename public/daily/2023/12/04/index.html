Highlights

  • Best-effort notebook support now typechecks top-level statements as the body of a synthetic async function. This restores completions and ordinary type errors around top-level await, and initializes the same implicit capability locals as a function instead of reporting $#capability as undefined. Normal Hack files still receive the general error that top-level statements are unsupported. aa87ac15ee d5c48abf18
