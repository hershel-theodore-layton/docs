Highlights

  • Repo-authoritative builds can use repeatable --static-pattern glob options to include only selected files in file.cache; this mode cannot be combined with --cdir or --cfile. 51c53a7882 bd70a8f60a
  • HSL adds HH\Lib\Async\LowPri<T> for starting non-critical async work at low priority and promoting it later. run() has callee requirements [ctx $action, write_props], and prioritize() requires [write_props], so their callers must provide at least those capabilities. b90e9d07ac
  • The HHI for PriorityBridgeWaitHandle::prioritize() now explicitly uses [] instead of omitted brackets. This weakens its callee requirement from implicit [defaults] to no capabilities and is therefore non-breaking for callers. b90e9d07ac
