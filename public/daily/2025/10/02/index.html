Highlights

  • Typing[4104] and Typing[4105] now say "too few positional arguments" and "too many positional arguments." Named-argument arity has separate diagnostics, so the revised wording identifies the part of a call that is actually wrong. 9a285f64ab

Future Changes

  • Unstable named parameters now participate in function-type subtyping and override arity checks. A subtype must accept every named parameter promised by its supertype and may not make an optional named parameter required. a354304453
