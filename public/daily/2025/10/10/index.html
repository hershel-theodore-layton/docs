Security

  • Servers can set Server.AllowExec=false to disable process-launching APIs. HHVM then throws for shell_exec(), exec(), passthru(), system(), proc_open(), pcntl_exec(), and HSL's OS\fork_and_execve(); the default remains true. 5a71f2f16a

Future Changes

  • The staged null-safe pipe operator |?> evaluates its right-hand side only when the left-hand side is non-null. Within the right-hand side $$ is narrowed to non-null, and the whole expression remains nullable. 1ba856e4ee
