Highlights

  • The HH\autoload_*_to_path(), HH\autoload_path_to_*(), and HH\autoload_is_native() declarations now explicitly require [] instead of their former implicit [defaults]. This narrows their caller requirements, allowing these deterministic autoload lookups from pure contexts; it is a capability relaxation, not a breaking change. 5c73ddb336
  • Runtime is checks against a reified shape or tuple generic now first verify that the value has the matching type-structure kind, preventing a segfault when the actual value has a different kind. 9f0e9f6d43
