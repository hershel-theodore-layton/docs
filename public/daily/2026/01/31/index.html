Highlights

  • New pure HH\Sandbox\dict_with_capacity() and HH\Sandbox\vec_with_capacity() helpers return empty arrays with a requested capacity hint, reducing reallocations while populating a known-size collection. Capacity must be positive; HHVM may cap or ignore the hint, and repo-authoritative mode returns a normal empty array. 0e7715a0fc c61c8ad0da

The accessors and pack() retain []; __debugInfo() still omits a capability list and therefore requires [defaults]. 095cbba3b9
