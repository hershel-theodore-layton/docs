Breaking Changes

  • ReflectionMethod::invoke() and invokeArgs() are now deprecated in Hack declarations. Migrate reflective call sites to HH_FIXME::reflectionMethodInvokeForCallsite() and HH_FIXME::reflectionMethodInvokeArgsForCallsite() while removing avoidable reflection. 5d4b0335e7

Future Changes

  • Preview expression trees can now desugar vec[...], dict[...], and keyset[...] literals through visitVec, visitDict, and visitKeyset. The support is gated by expression_tree_hack_arrays. b8379a88ac
  • The staged named-parameter runtime now has dedicated function entries for optional named parameters and can initialize their defaults consistently in the interpreter and JIT, including coeffect checks after initialization. Named parameters are not yet Released. 4214c0a6a3 e313bccd11 585ae3ac58 ff5c55a8d2
