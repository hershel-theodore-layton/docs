Highlights

  • serialize() accepts an options dictionary, including zstd=true; HH\serialize_with_options() and unserialize() also support streaming zstd compression or decompression. This avoids materializing the full uncompressed serialized string at once. 35e214ff7a
  • HH\ImplicitContext\PreparedContext::runBatch() and runBatchAsync() no longer fail with a coeffect violation before running callbacks with [] or [zoned_shallow] contexts. Their shared preparation helper now declares [leak_safe]. 1d894fd5cc

Breaking Changes

  • Files assigned to implicit packages by their paths may no longer use __PackageOverride to escape that membership. 51094a61e4
