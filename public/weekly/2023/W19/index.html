Week 19: May 8–May 14, 2023

14 noteworthy changes, organized by the source changelog sections.

DayWeekMonth
←Previous week→Next week

Highlights

7 changes
  • Mon, May 8
    PACKAGES.toml now contributes to the project configuration hash alongside .hhconfig, so saved-state and configuration consumers can detect package-definition changes. 6385f479a8
  • Mon, May 8
    The VS Code debugger can now evaluate await expressions at its prompt; debugger-generated async code is recognized as a special function context rather than rejected as top-level await. 1843538b7f
  • Tue, May 9
    A new “flip around comma” code action swaps adjacent arguments, parameters, collection elements, shape fields, and similar comma-separated items. a9aa1df6ee
  • Wed, May 10
    Sound Dynamic hover now handles more expression and declaration forms correctly. Structured JSON from --type-at-pos hides migration-only like-type wrappers while the display string retains them, and redundant HH\FIXME\UNSAFE_CAST_NONNULL casts can now be removed by the quick-fix and codemod. 9cf23265f2 ccf7af145a 95353318c3
  • Thu, May 11
    Sound Dynamic hover results no longer depend on whether another IDE request populated the TAST cache first. Cached ordinary and dynamic-assumption TASTs are now distinguished. e2b26e3bbc
  • Thu, May 11
    The IDE now offers an “inline variable” refactor for replacing a local variable with its initializer and removing the declaration when safe. a37c556428
  • Fri, May 12
    Sound Dynamic checking now recognizes <<__ReturnDisposable>> through ~ and supportdyn wrappers, closing a hole where disposable-return rules could be bypassed. 5332fadec7

Breaking Changes

5 changes
  • Tue, May 9
    XHP attribute access now typechecks the underlying getAttribute() call, including the method's signature and required capabilities. Custom XHP bases with an incompatible or missing method may receive new errors where the attribute is read. c6b3bc7f59
  • Wed, May 10
    Source use of <<__NoAutoDynamic>> now requires enable_no_auto_dynamic = true; HHI declarations remain exempt. da86d4290f
  • Wed, May 10
    Reserved type-name checks now apply uniformly to class, interface, trait, enum, enum-class, and type-alias declarations, including declarations inside namespaces. varray and darray are explicitly unavailable as alias names, while classname is now allowed as a user-defined classish or enum name. Direct HackC users may newly see parse errors for names that hh already rejected. 3ee812d373 8f41d4f642 76a42d2d94 da292e9950 ade4fad16e
  • Thu, May 11
    <<__NoAutoDynamic>> is no longer accepted on classes. Apply the opt-out to supported declarations such as individual methods or interfaces instead. 60560ff1fd
  • Fri, May 12
    User-defined autoload maps and autoload_set_paths() support have been removed. Sandbox deployments use native Facts autoloading, while repo-authoritative deployments use RepoAutoloadMap. 6bb7530a87

Future Changes

2 changes
  • Tue, May 9
    Runtime validation for <<__ModuleLevelTrait>> requires the annotated trait to be public and permits it to use only other module-level traits. Invalid combinations fail consistently before optimized execution. d38803e101
  • Sun, May 14
    Preview readonly checking now examines every callable in a union of function types. A call is rejected if any possible target is non-readonly, closing a soundness hole that was particularly visible through like-function types. fb3f1c41ca
