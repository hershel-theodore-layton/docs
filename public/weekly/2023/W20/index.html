Week 20: May 15–May 21, 2023

13 noteworthy changes, organized by the source changelog sections.

DayWeekMonth
←Previous week→Next week

Highlights

7 changes
  • Mon, May 15
    Switch-exhaustiveness diagnostics list at most ten missing cases, in declaration order, instead of attempting to render every case and potentially crashing on large enums. 5c78876f00
  • Mon, May 15
    A successful equality comparison now refines each operand with the other's type. For example, inside $value === 42, a ?int value is known to be int without a separate non-null assertion. ec4f5b16e0
  • Wed, May 17
    The IDE now offers “Extract into method” for expressions and statement selections, including async functions, iterators, parameters, return values, and relevant modifiers. 3614df0797
  • Thu, May 18
    “Extract into method” now creates an instance method by default, unless the selection comes from a static method. This makes extracted helpers easier to override or mock. 4a1362a0da
  • Thu, May 18
    Runtime type-structure resolution detects recursive type aliases instead of recursing until HHVM crashes. f8d0f2f777
  • Fri, May 19
    BuiltinEnum APIs are now marked __NoAutoDynamic, removing unnecessary like-types from methods whose implementations are known to preserve their declared generic types. b3df928ba8
  • Fri, May 19
    Subtyping diagnostics involving a union with dynamic now prefer the incompatible non-dynamic member, producing more useful errors for ordinary expressions such as arithmetic. 7b5b19c496

Breaking Changes

2 changes
  • Mon, May 15
    The obsolete hh --coverage and hh --color commands, along with the old IDE coverage status, have been removed. They predated constraint-based typechecking and no longer represented meaningful type coverage. 10a04c1344
  • Mon, May 15
    Enabling implicit Sound Dynamic no longer implicitly enables the supportdyn type-hint syntax. Projects writing supportdyn<T> directly must explicitly enable enable_supportdyn_hint; generated pessimised declarations are unaffected. 126a09182e

Future Changes

4 changes
  • Tue, May 16
    Experimental case types now have runtime type structures. type_structure(), reflection, and is/as checks can distinguish case-type aliases by name while inspecting their variants. Parameter and return enforcement is not yet part of this change. e2f3af435a
  • Wed, May 17
    Preview readonly checks now look through like-types when identifying Hack arrays, and required-XHP-attribute analysis handles compound like-types more completely. 045810ce56 f9a431a880
  • Wed, May 17
    Sound Dynamic no longer enables the experimental upcast expression automatically. Projects using upcast syntax must opt into upcast_expression independently. 0620b140c2
  • Sat, May 20
    Runtime method lookup now preserves the defining module of methods copied from module-level traits, so their internal accesses are checked against the trait's module rather than the consuming class's module. Runtime validation also rejects public traits that declare internal methods or properties, matching the existing Hack check. dd90abb638 dc0a3ac672
