Week 19: May 5–May 11, 2025

7 noteworthy changes, organized by the source changelog sections.

Highlights

1 change
  • Tue, May 6
    Visibility errors for a protected or private override of an internal member now report the actual narrowing mismatch instead of incorrectly saying that the parent member is inaccessible from its own module. 78719d189d

Breaking Changes

4 changes
  • Tue, May 6
    HH\TypeStructure<T> now models return_type and param_types as optional fields, matching the runtime values. Code that reads either field directly must first test for it or use ?? or Shapes::idx(). 472f99c64d 5e4e71971c
  • Wed, May 7
    HH\TypeStructure<T> now models elem_types, allows_unknown_fields, is_cls_cns, and optional_shape_field as optional fields rather than always-present nullable fields. Use an optional-shape access before consuming them. e042434722 89362e19c3
  • Thu, May 8
    HH\TypeStructure<T> now models nullable and name as optional fields. Their present values are non-null, but callers must account for the keys being absent. 742f8167f1
  • Fri, May 9
    HH\TypeStructure<T> now models value, typevars, and alias as optional fields. Direct indexing that assumed those keys were always present must move to an optional-shape access. 4bd21c5ee9

Security

1 change
  • Thu, May 8
    The Hack toolchain's Tokio dependency has been upgraded from 1.41.0 to 1.45.0 to address SNYK-RUST-TOKIO-9804284. a950001d30

Future Changes

1 change
  • Fri, May 9
    The checker now rejects <<__Memoize>> and <<__MemoizeLSB>> on functions whose parameter list uses the unstable type-splat syntax. Such declarations cannot currently produce a valid memoization wrapper. c86642dbcc
