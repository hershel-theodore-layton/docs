Week 19: May 4–May 10, 2026

8 noteworthy changes, organized by the source changelog sections.

DayWeekMonth
←Previous week→Next week

Highlights

1 change
  • Mon, May 4
    The class manual now gives a consolidated account of public, protected, private, internal, and protected internal, including constructor-promoted properties and visibility rules for overrides. e750dcbdf6

Breaking Changes

2 changes
  • Tue, May 5
    ReflectionMethod::invoke() and invokeArgs() are now deprecated in Hack declarations. Migrate reflective call sites to HH_FIXME::reflectionMethodInvokeForCallsite() and HH_FIXME::reflectionMethodInvokeArgsForCallsite() while removing avoidable reflection. 5d4b0335e7
  • Thu, May 7
    __AutocompleteSortText and its rollout configuration have been removed. The attribute no longer influences IDE completion ordering, and projects should delete it rather than relying on custom sort prefixes. 912edca7b3

Future Changes

4 changes
  • Tue, May 5
    Preview expression trees can now desugar vec[...], dict[...], and keyset[...] literals through visitVec, visitDict, and visitKeyset. The support is gated by expression_tree_hack_arrays. b8379a88ac
  • Tue, May 5
    The staged named-parameter runtime now has dedicated function entries for optional named parameters and can initialize their defaults consistently in the interpreter and JIT, including coeffect checks after initialization. Named parameters are not yet Released. 4214c0a6a3 e313bccd11 585ae3ac58 ff5c55a8d2
  • Wed, May 6
    Sealed methods no longer require an unstable-feature declaration at their use site. The feature is still classified as OngoingRelease rather than Released, so projects should continue to treat it as pre-stable. 2017c9b602
  • Wed, May 6
    Named parameters moved from Unstable to Preview in Hack's feature registry. This broadens the intended testing audience but does not make their syntax or behavior stable. 8d1b4f3f5d

Security

1 change
  • Fri, May 8
    Hack's Rust rand dependency was updated from 0.8.5 to 0.8.6 to address RUSTSEC-2026-0097. 30e51d9aa3
