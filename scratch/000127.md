Commit title: make const-cast explicit in Expected
Commit body:
Summary:
In the case of `Expected<V const, E>`, initialization and emplacement of a value may use placement-new with the address as `void*`. Rather than use the C-style syntax `(void*)std::addressof(value)`, use only C++-style syntax.

Notes: We must use `void*` and not `void const*` since placement-new requires `T*` for `T` not-`const`. We must use `void*` and not any other `T*` since placement-new may be overridden per-type, but `Expected` does not support placement-new overrides. We must use `std::addressof` since we have typed storage, and the type may overload free `operator&` or may define member `operator&`.
