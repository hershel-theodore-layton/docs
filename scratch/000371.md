Commit title: Fix Tile leak on shutdown
Commit body:
Summary:
Currently, we may leak Interactions during shutdown. The sequence is as follows:
* During the shutdown, ThriftServer would initiate draining of all Interactions (Tiles) [here](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[5b383c84174a]/fbcode/thrift/lib/cpp2/async/AsyncProcessor.cpp?lines=197-213)
* The last for loop in the function would release the TilePtrs from another thread, which would schedule the event on another EventBase [here]( https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[5b383c84174a]/fbcode/thrift/lib/cpp2/async/Interaction.cpp?lines=172-173).
* Once the above task runs on the other EB, we would try to schedule a decref on the ThreadManager [here](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[3bc2b88a11ae]/fbcode/thrift/lib/cpp2/async/Interaction.cpp?lines=52).
* However, the `KeepAlive` we hold is a "dummy" (i.e. it doesn't prevent Executor from shutting down). And in our case, the actual Threads are past joining keepalives and would simply discard the task. And since we're capturing raw pointer, no destruction actually happens.

This diff changes the raw pointer to a `unique_ptr` to ensure destruction actually happens.
