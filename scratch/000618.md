Commit title: Prevent destruction of RequestContext::get()'s static defaultContext
Commit body:
Summary:
References to `defaultContext` returned by `RequestContext::get()` can/are
leaked to objects that persist beyond shutdown (`exit`).  But `static` objects
like `defaultContext` are destroyed at `exit()` time.  This can result in weird
use-after-frees while `exit()` is running.  Instead, do not destruct
`defaultContext` at shutdown.
