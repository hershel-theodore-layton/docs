Commit title: Rename Value::is_immediate to is_int
Commit body:
Summary: In the hackathon which started ocamlrep, I borrowed the word "immediate" from [this Real World OCaml chapter](https://dev.realworldocaml.org/runtime-memory-layout.html#:~:text=An%20OCaml%20value%20is%20a,they%20are%20no%20longer%20needed.) to mean "nonpointer tagged value". But this is an odd term to use; [ocaml just uses "int" or "long"](https://github.com/ocaml/ocaml/blob/trunk/runtime/caml/mlvalues.h#L69-L80), [we use "int" elsewhere in ocamlrep](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[651ffc3433cc]/fbcode/hphp/hack/src/ocamlrep/value.rs?lines=58), and "immediate" has other meanings in the context of compiler/vm implementation. Rename to `is_int` and abandon the term "immediate".
