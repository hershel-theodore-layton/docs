Commit title: Further decompose hint elaboration
Commit body:
Summary:
The hint elaboration pass is one of the more intricate naming passes, performing all of the following :
- canoncalising of `Happly` hints coming from lowering
- validating uses of `void` and `noreturn` hints
- validating uses of wildcard hints and rewriting to `Herr`
- validating uses of `Hlike` hints
- elaborating & validating of class constant shape field names
- validating uses of `Hthis` hints and rewriting to `Herr`
- validating root hints inside `Haccess` and rewriting to `Herr`
- validating hints used in `Cast` expressions

This diff breaks the pass into these constituent parts
