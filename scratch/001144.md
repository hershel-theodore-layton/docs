Commit title: Treat private methods in SDT classes as SDT
Commit body:
Summary: Currently we only check *private* methods in SDT classes as though they are called statically i.e. not through `dynamic`. But we permit such methods to be called through `dynamic` or through like types. This is unsound. For now, strengthen the check so that `private` and non-`private` methods are checked similarly, requiring that they be callable dynamically.
