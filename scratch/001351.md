Summary:
`find_by_ordinal` and `find_by_field_id` are completely broken in a nondeterministic, hard to debug way because their implementation, `for_each_ordinal_impl`, fails to initialize its result. Stumbled upon this issue when trying to debug test failures in D42147642.
