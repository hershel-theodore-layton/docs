Commit title: Add RustProviderBackend::shallow_decl_provider
Commit body:
Summary:
Require implementors of the RustProviderBackend trait to provide a shallow_decl_provider method. In practice, every implementor already has a shallow decl provider.

Historically, hh_server stored in sharedmem shallow funs/consts/modules and folded classes only, making it infeasible to provide shallow classes inexpensively. I omitted shallow_decl_provider from RustProviderBackend with this design in mind. But after the populate_member_heaps=false rollout, we do store shallow classes in sharedmem, and it doesn't make sense to constrain ourselves with that old design in mind anymore.
