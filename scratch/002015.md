Summary:
The immediates may be unsigned and larger than the maximum signed value.  In
such cases, Immed::b() and Immed::w() would throw because they use safe_cast.
Replace these with Immed::ub() and Immed::uw(), which use static_cast instead
and work for getting both signed and unsigned values.
