Summary:
LSP distinguishes between `textEdit` (the primary edit) and `additionalTextEdits`.

https://microsoft.github.io/language-server-protocol/specifications/lsp/3.17/specification/#completionItem

These behave differently: `textEdit` is affected by `textEditFormat` but `additionalTextEdits` is not.

Update the record fields returned by autocompleteService to reflect this, and always return a `textEdit`.
