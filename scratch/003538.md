Commit title: Fix some `ReadAncillaryDataCallback` footguns
Commit body:
Summary:
This fixes a few issues:
 - New docstrings clarify the not-so-simple contract that implementors of `ReadAncillaryDataCallback` must adhere to.
 - Make it super-clear that `ancillaryData()` is expected check for `MSG_CTRUNC`, and explain why we do not do so in `AsyncSocket`.
 - Invoke `ancillaryData()` in such a way that if it were to close the underlying `AsyncSocket`, it wouldn't cause a state-handling bug & crash due to `readCallback_` becoming null.
 - The child classes of `AsyncSocket` that reimplement `performRead*` should `DCHECK` that `readAncillaryDataCallback_` is null, since none of them support it.

I also added a test to verify some of the expected contracts (failing / closing from `ancillaryData`, and `MSG_CTRUNC` behavior).
