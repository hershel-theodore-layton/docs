Commit title: Add heuristic for like-subtyping
Commit body:
Summary: If we have a `newtype T<T1> as f(T1)` then subtyping `T<t> <: ~T<u>` should just consider `T<t> <: T<u>` and not try to expand the bound on `T<t>` to `f(t) <: T<u>` or `f(t) <: dynamic`. The first isn't helpful unless `T<u>` has a lower bound. While it is possible for the second to be necessary, in practice the extra disjunction seems to get in the way far more often then it helps.
