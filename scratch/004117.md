Summary:
Adds limitUsage (i.e. current active requests or QPS) to the CPUConcurrencyController::EventHandler::onCycle callback.

With this change, `getLimitUsage()` is always called once at the beginning of `cycleOnce()`. This ensures that the QPS tracking logic executed when running in TOKEN_BUCKET mode is refreshed every cycle (importantly, at the same cadence as the load is measured).
