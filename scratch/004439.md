Commit title: Introduce a bias when checking `T < ~U` towards the inner error
Commit body:
Summary:
For `T <: (U | ... | V)` we have a special case that says if you can strip a dynamic out of the union and get a singleton type `W`, we do `T <: W ||| T <: dynamic`, but our typing error represented by `~fail` is still pointing to the outer types. This introduces a bias towards the result of `T <: W` if we find that `T <: dynamic` has failed.

This is morally similar to {D44964768}
