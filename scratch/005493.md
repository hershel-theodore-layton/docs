Commit body:
Summary:
Pos.t ranges are exclusive at the end, e.g. this range will have start-col 0 and end-col 3.
```
ABC
^..^
```

I think it's senseless and confusing that nuclide_rpc_message_printer produces ranges that are inclusive at the end! The above example will have start-col 0 and end-col 2.

I encountered this while porting GoToImpl tests from hh_server to ide_standalone. They were returning 2-character ranges when the symbol is 3-characters long.
