Commit title: "add null-safe get" code action
Commit body:
Summary:
When `$a->prop` fails due to left-hand-side that might be null, offer the user to replace with `$a?->prop`.

**Choices**

- I used the term "null-safe-get" because [the `?->` operator does not seem to be named in our docs](https://docs.hhvm.com/hack/expressions-and-operators/member-selection). And in our AST it's just `get` with a flag.
- We do not offer the code action when the receiver and the rhs of `$obj->foo` are on different lines. This is because (afaict) we have no way of finding where the `->` is in such cases, due to limitations in the tast representation.
