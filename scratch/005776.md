Commit title: Do not consider Enter as an UnconditionalBranch
Commit body:
Summary:
If a call is intercepted, `Enter` does not jump to `targetpc`. CTI needs to use an indirect jump after interpreting `Enter` bytecode.
If the call is *not* intercepted (common case), `Enter` jumps to the `targetpc`, which is always the first instruction of the function, therefore `lookup_cti` does not need to take the slow path.
