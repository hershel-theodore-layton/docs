Commit title: use only relaxed ops for packed isset bitsets
Commit body:
Summary: These isset bits do not guard concurrent access to the corresponding fields. It is expected that the application will provide its own synchronization mechanisms where needed. The primary requirement here is ensuring that concurrent operations to two different isset bits are done as-if one after the other with respect to each other, but nothing more. Here, the operations with non-relaxed memory-orders were the copy-constructors and copy-assignment operators; these are less common but nevertheless should also be relaxed.
