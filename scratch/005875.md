Summary:
We have two known unhandled exceptions named `Decl_not_found` and `Decl_heap_elems_bug`. They get caught at top level and turned into their own special Exit_status code and reported in TYPECHECK_EXIT. Our telemetry specifically filters out these two codes because we've never had a plan to address them.

With this diff, I'm making them also report DECL_CONSISTENCY_BUG to scuba. This will be a touch richer (e.g. have callstack of where the problem arose) and will also be nice to see together with the other DECL_CONSISTENCY_BUGs.

I also reworded the lsp error message associated with them. This is so the scuba column "Lsp_error_message" can be purely category, and I can leave more precise data to another column. At the moment, Lsp_error_message combines category and data: https://fburl.com/scuba/hh_server_events/s5x2uh8a
