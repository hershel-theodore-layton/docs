The Deprecated attribute doesn't work on constants, so this just deletes the STDIO constants.

Why? they aren't real constants; they compile into [effectively] plain function
calls. Deleting them allows us to rip out the weird bespoke logic in HHVM that supports them. Their replacements, like HH\stdin(), reflect their true nature.
