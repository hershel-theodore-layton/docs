Commit title: add a mutator to associate const_type to const_value for structured annotations.
Commit body:
Summary:
Currently we have two mutators doing similar jobs:
  1. `const_type_to_const_value` for regular constants defined in IDL
  2. `field_type_to_const_value` for field default value const_value
These two mutators won't process const_values introduced by structured annotations (e.g. those in `thrift/compiler/test/fixtures/adapter/src/with_containers.thrift`). Here we add a new mutator (i.e. structured_annotation_type_to_const_value)  for those values.
