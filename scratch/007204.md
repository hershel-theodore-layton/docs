Commit title: Recover exhaustivity check in the presence of intersected like types
Commit body:
Summary:
When an enum is pessimised we get a type of the form `bound & ~E` where `bound` is the visible bound of `E`.

Because exhaustivity check is partial when it comes to intersections, `bound` part of the intersection can turn of exhaustivity checking which turns of useful signal on enums.

This diff recovers it by exempting specifically this pattern.
