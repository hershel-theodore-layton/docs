Summary:
The thrift decoder can be caused to crash if given bad input.

- Fix VecInit to check that the given capacity doesn't exceed the max probe size
- Fix thrift decoder to only reserve 16k entries at a time instead of trying to pre-allocate the maximum specified size.  This should be a reasonable trade-off between performance (small sized arrays will preallocate the entire size) and utility (large arrays will verify that the data is actually serialized as it goes along).
