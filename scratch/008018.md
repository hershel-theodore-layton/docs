Commit title: Remove problematic annotations
Commit body:
Summary:
`Deprecated` and `Legacy` annotations have been removed. Remove the corresponding `NoDeprecated` and `NoLegacy` annotations as well. An explicit opt out from legacy/deprecated features is an anti-pattern. In particular:

1. It doesn't solve the problem for the vast majority of users.
2. It causes API/language bifurcation.
3. Once such annotations are used it is pretty much impossible to deprecate new things as we know from experience with `-Werror`.

There was ~zero adoption of these annotations so we can safely remove them.

As a nice side effect this removes a whopping 80 kLOC of noise from fixtures.
