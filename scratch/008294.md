Now that we've committed to the batch indexer, this deletes some dead code paths, in particular `ClientIdeDaemon.{should_process_file_change,process_one_file_change}`

This also means that the scenarios around "reporting partial progress of indexing" are now useless. This diff deletes the associated messages. **Before:**
1. After initialization, we send `Done_init(index_remaining)`
2. While indexing (either after initialization or after DidChangeWatchedFiles) we send `Processing(index_remaining)`
3. After indexing (in either scenario) we send `Done_processing`

**After this diff:**
1. After initialization, we send `Done_init()`.

Because of these changes, the concept of `changed_files_denominator` is no longer needed.
