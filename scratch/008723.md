Commit title: Change most usages of t_struct to t_structured
Commit body:
Summary:
Most clients use pointers and reference to `t_struct`, even though they actually should be using `t_structured`. Up until now, the difference is mainly theoretical (because `t_structured` is abstract and `t_struct` is its only subclass), but as we decouple unions, exceptions, etc. from `t_struct` (by making them derive `t_structured`), the distinction becomes relevant and important.

Most previous diffs in this stack update usages for each decoupled use case, so in this diff we simply convert the remaining callers to facilitate future decoupling.
