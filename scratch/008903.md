Commit title: Fix pathological behaviors of compute_available_stores()
Commit body:
Summary:
compute_available_stores() performs dataflow on the CFG and at each edge
propagation, it tries to fully recalculate the TrackedStore from all
predecessor blocks, each of them recursively finding a candidate store, which
can potentially traverse a significant portion of the CFG. This all happens per
each potentially available store.

Let's keep track of the candidate store for phis in TrackedStore. Let's also
not recalculate the new TrackedStore from all predecessors at the edge dataflow
step, do it instead at the node step.
