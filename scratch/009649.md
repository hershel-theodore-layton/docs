Commit title: Add latch synchronization primitive to HPHP::coro
Commit body:
Summary:
Latch is a synchronization primitive which lets all coroutines block
until they all reach the latch, then release them all. For emulated
coroutines there's a bit of an issue because you cannot really "block"
without deadlocking. In that case, Latch is just a no-op, so must be
used with care.
