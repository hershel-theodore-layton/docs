Summary:
My goal for this diffstack is to redo the way ClientIdeIncremental, Provider_utils.quarantine and Provider_utils.invalidate work. (1) Users currently experience bugs due to them not invalidating the necessary decls. (2) They invalidate unnecessary decls, which I suspect hurts perf.

---------

I wish to use position-insensitive decl hashes, `FileInfo.pfh_hash`. My idea is that if the user types something inside a method body, then signatures won't change, and therefore we needn't invalidate any shallow or folded decls that are already cached, nor any TASTs. There are two places we'll need `pfh_hash` further up this stack:
1. In `respect_but_quarantine`, e.g. upon DidOpen or DidChange, where we're given updated source text, we do a direct-decl-parse on it, and compare the `pfh_hash` that came out of the direct-decl-parse with what our decl-store has.
2. Upon disk change, the multithreaded rust indexer runs, and produces `FileInfo.change` for each file-change, and we'll need to get `pfh_hash` out of that as well. So we can compare the new disk content with what our decl cache is storing: if the new disk content doesn't affect any decls, then we won't need to invalidate.

This diff solely does plumbing to support scenario (2): it gets the `pfh_hash` out of rust-batch-indexer and into `FileInfo.change`.
