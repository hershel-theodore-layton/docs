Commit title: Kill MIState at BeginCatch instead of EndCatch
Commit body:
Summary:
HHVM bytecode does not support recovery of an exception in the middle of a
minstr sequence. Currently, MIState is killed implicitly by the EndCatch
opcode, plus explicitly when side-exiting from a catch block due to
InvalidSetMException. Let's instead kill it implicitly at BeginCatch.

This preserves the original behavior, even with the next diff when exceptions
get routed to exception handlers instead of EndCatch.

While there, remove incorrect MIState kill from JmpExit memory effects. We
don't currently have any MIState at places where JmpExit is used, but in theory
we could in the future.
