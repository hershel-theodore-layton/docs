Summary:
D39310365 (Sep 2022) had moved Decl_provider.get_typedef into a separate module.

Conceptually this is odd. That's because typedef, const, fun and module are all equivalent things, and what applies to one of them should apply to all of them. This diff moves them all uniformly into Decl_provider_internals. Let me spell out:

1. `Decl_provider` is a weird combination of folded-class-decls and shallow-non-class-decls. I wish it weren't, but there it is. (Shallow-class-decls go in a separate Shallow_classes_provider.)
2. Its responsibility is (1) manage the folded-class-decls, (2) pessimise funs and typedefs where needed, (3) do decl-counter telemetry, (4) delegate to Decl_provider_internals the work of fetching shallow-non-class-decls.
3. `Decl_provider_internals` is a lower-level module whose responsibility is solely to compute+cache shallow-non-class-decls.

The dependency problem which D39310365 was trying to solve is that decl_provider depends upon pessimise depends upon decl_enforceability, which in turn depends upon decl_provider_internals. Prior to D39310365, decl_provider and decl_provider_internals were one and the same, which naturally didn't work!
