Summary:
I'm trying to simplify the providers.

There's an oddball function `Shallow_classes_provider.get_batch`. All it does is call `get_shallow_class` for a set of names. And it has only a single caller. I reckon it has no good reason to exist, so I'm inlining it into its caller.

There's another oddball function `Shallow_classes_provider.remove_batch`. Look at its unique caller, and its implementation:
```
let Decl_redecl_service.remove_defs ... =
  match Provider_backend.get () with
  | Provider_backend.Rust_provider_backend be ->
    Rust_provider_backend.Decl.remove_defs be names
  | _ ->
    Shallow_classes_provider.remove_batch ctx n_classes;
    Decl_heap.Classes.remove_batch n_classes;
    ... and other shmem heap deletions

let Shallow_classes_remover.remove_batch ... =
  | Provider_backend.Analysis
  | Provider_backend.Rust_provider_backend _
  | Provider_backend.Shared_memory ->
    Shallow_classes_heap.Classes.remove_batch names
  | _ -> failwith
```

Decl_redecl_service is only used by ServerTypeCheck.ml and ServerLazyInit.ml, i.e. hh_server, hence only Shared_memory backend previously, and Rust_provider_backend now. (That's why we don't hit any of the failwith cases). I reckon it's clearer to inline it into its caller. That's what this diff does.

I think on the surface it looked like `Shallow_classes_provider.get_batch` and `remove_batch` belonged together in a nice kind of symmetry. But under the hood they didn't really.
