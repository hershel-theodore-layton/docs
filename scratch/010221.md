Summary:
This diff fixes two irritations with hh_single_type_check:
1. When you do `hh_single_type_check --root ~/www ...` it used to require you to specify the path to a naming-table. And you'd always just look in /tmp/hh_server/hh_saved_states_sql to pick one at random.
   1. This diff removes the tedium by doing that search for you, and picking the first one it finds.
2. When you do `hh_single_type_check --root ~/www ...` then the defaults in hh_single_type_check (for enable_sound_dynamic and everything_sdt) differed, so hhstc would give errors which hh in WWW didn't.
   1. This diff makes hhstc pick up the www/.hhconfig values for `enable_sound_dynamic` and `everything_sdt`
