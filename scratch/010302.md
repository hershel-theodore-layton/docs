Commit title: address too many suggestions with enumclass+refinement types
Commit body:
Summary:
The autocomplete for enum class labels rely on subtype to identify the matching constants.  In presence of refinement types unresolved variables may be present in the Tast types, and the existing call to `is_sub_type` returns `false` if some constraints are left after subtype simplification.  As a result, on the minimal repro `enum_class_label_with_refined_types.php`, the autocomplete could not find any matching constant, and returned all of them (including those who do not satisfy the tyep constraints).

By replacing `is_sub_type` with `sub_type` we ensure that the constraints generated by subtype simplification are solved, causing the autocomplete to suggest only the correct completions.
