Summary:
I realized we still do a bunch of extra intersections in `aggregate_data`

Two optimizations:
- (1) (This diff) Avoid iterating over all the children once we determine we have computed all the children we need already.
- (2) (Next diff) Topologically sort the children
   - This was not obvious to me last week. But topologically sorting the children is strictly better than just partitioning the children by "top". This is most easily seen by considering the case where our children are A and B, where A is a parent of B, and both are top nodes. Supposed B is before A in our list. We should just do 1 intersection with A, instead of intersecting with both A and B.
