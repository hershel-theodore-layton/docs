Summary:
The `xplat/proxygen:utils_time` and `proxygen/lib/utils:time_util` are used widely, but they have dependency on [openssl](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[c718fa57b368aaa0fc0c28e13b456d88ed6cd4c9]/xplat/proxygen/BUCK?lines=2030). The dependency is from `getDateTimeStr(const ASN1_TIME* const time)` which is only used by [proxygen/facebook/lib/ssl/Cert.cpp](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[2c06e449ba81b8651e644acd411cc79b145125e2]/xplat/proxygen/facebook/lib/ssl/Cert.cpp?lines=50%2C58).

We could move the `getDateTimeStr(const ASN1_TIME* const time)` to where it is [used](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[2c06e449ba81b8651e644acd411cc79b145125e2]/xplat/proxygen/facebook/lib/ssl/Cert.cpp?lines=50%2C58). It can reduce the number of BUCK targets that may depend on `openssl`.

In summary, these are the changes:
* Remove `xplat/proxygen/lib/utils/Time.cpp`.
* Move the only function inside `xplat/proxygen/lib/utils/Time.cpp` to `Cert.cpp`.
* Remove the `openssl` dependency in `xplat/proxygen:utils_time` and `proxygen/lib/utils:time_util`.
* Update `fbcode/fbpkg/tracker/TARGETS` as it implicitly depends on `openssl`.
* Update `fbcode/proxygen/lib/CMakeLists.txt` for removing of `xplat/proxygen/lib/utils/Time.cpp`.
