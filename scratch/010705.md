Summary:
I was surprised and dismayed that my first attempt at sticky_quarantine made perf a bit worse.

This diff adds telemetry about the cost of invalidating decls. I hope that this way there won't be similar surprises.
* I added telemetry into the `hh_compute_tast_ide` table, a table that already includes Tast_provider.compute_tast events from the IDE. I figure that quarantine and invalidation have more or less the same frequency and needs for logging.
* The three events are now COMPUTE_TAST, INVALIDATE, QUARANTINE

I also added a new field `lsp_tracking_id`. This will let us correlate any of these three events from this table, with the CLIENT_LSP_METHOD event in hh_server_events, and also with `activity_id` in vscode_chronicle_language_server_events. This way I hope to see how much invalidation and compute-tast contribute to slowness.
