Commit title: replace uses of FOLLY_CXX17_CONSTEXPR with constexpr
Commit body:
Summary:
With C++17 at minimum required across all compilers, `FOLLY_CXX17_CONSTEXPR` would evaluate unconditionally as `constexpr` anyway. So simplify.

This was typically used to optimize compile speed in cases of `if` with a constant condition and expensive-to-compile code on one or the other side of the condition.
