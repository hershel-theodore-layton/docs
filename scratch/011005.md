Commit title: Fix for incompleteness related to like-pushing and nullables
Commit body:
Summary: When applying subtyping such as `vec<~int> <: ~#1`, we look through the bounds of `#1` in case there is some like-pushing that can be applied e.g. here, if we have `vec<int> <: #1 <: vec<int>`. Unfortunately we miss an opportunity if a nullable has been split into two lower bounds e.g. `null, vec<int> <: #1 <: ?vec<int>`. Let's make this a special case.
