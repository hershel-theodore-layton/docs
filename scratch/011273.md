## Change

This diff is a fairly straightforward port of the NativeImpl and JIT implementation of `HH\classname_to_class` to use the `ClassGetC` bytecode.

- HackC rewrites `HH\classname_to_class(<expr>)` into `emit_expr(expr); ClassGetC`
- **Definition moved to D53027662** Added an enum immediate to `ClassGetC` to signal whether the bytecode is emitted from `HH\class_to_classname` or through normal operations; for the former, we raise catchable InvalidArgumentExceptions instead of fataling and emit different notices
- Deleted PHP wrapper and supporting implementations
- Deleted the `emit{Module,Deployment}BoundaryViolationFrom` functions from {D51144984} as they are now dead
