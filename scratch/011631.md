## Approach

There is a dependency graph of OCaml files. And there's a much-coarser dependency graph of buck targets.
Showing the two graphs together and restricting the view to only relevant edges can help us see where to cut dependencies.
That is what `--output dot` is for.

Also, sometimes removing unused dependencies helps. That's what `--output unused` is for. Note that `--output unused` is overly aggressive right now.
