For now I have avoided removing the dependency of `:foo-rust`->`:foo-rust-clients` and `:foo-rust`->`:foo-rust-services`, in order to avoid thrashing autocargo if this diff needs to be reverted. I will let this stick for a day, then follow up on removing the unused dependencies.

After this diff, `:foo-rust` and `:foo-rust-types` will have nearly identical API, except for a `pub use self::errors::*`. The remaining work for the Thrift crate redesign is:

1. Temporarily add `pub use self::errors::*` into `:foo-rust-types` to make it fully interchangeable with `:foo-rust`.
2. Rename `:foo-rust-types` to `:foo-rust`. Add `:foo-rust-types` as an alias.
3. Codemod fbcode dependencies to change all `:foo-rust-types` dependencies to `:foo-rust`. There are not very many of these.
4. Delete `:foo-rust-types` alias.
5. (Optional; undecided) Codemod all uses of `foo::errors` to `foo_clients::errors` and `foo::services` to `foo_services::errors`. The use of top-level `services` module in the types crate to mean service errors, as opposed to service traits, is confusing.
6. Extract client mocks from `:foo-rust-clients` into a new `:foo-rust-mocks` crate that depends on `:foo-rust-clients`. Mocks account for much of the compile time of the clients crate but are rarely used.
