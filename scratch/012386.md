Commit title: Make CompressionCodecManager EventBase-local
Commit body:
Summary:
The codecs are associated to the worker, so they should be `EventBase`-local, not thread-local, in case the evb migrates threads.

`EventBaseLocal` is slightly more expensive than `thread_local`, but the overall cost of the `get{Client,Server}ContextInfo` functions is completely negligible (150$/year), so the overhead here will likely not even be measurable:
https://pxl.cl/4r0MW

See motivation here https://fb.prod.workplace.com/groups/143349833027145/posts/1305210553507728
