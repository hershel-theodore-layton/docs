ir::Func has a SrcLoc (embedded in its LocId table), while hhbc::Body has
a Span, which only preserves lines, not columns.  Since bc_to_ir can't supply
columns, and ir_to_bc cannot preserve columns, there's not much point to
ir::Func having a SrcLoc.

Change it to span, to decouple it from the LocId table used for instructions.
