Commit title: use ArcVec in ir::Immediate
Commit body:
Summary:
Flatten Immediate::Array(Arc<TypedValue>) into separate Vec, Dict, and
Keyset variants, holding ArcVec instead of TypedValue. This lines up better
with TypedValue, removes the unnecessary flexibility for Immediate::Array
to hold *any* TypedValue, and increases type safety by matching the
variants to the type of container they must hold.

After this, ir::Immediate is a strict superset of TypedValue.
