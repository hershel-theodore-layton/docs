Commit title: Make closures be owned by their declaring class in HHBBC
Commit body:
Summary:
Instead of treating closures as any other top-level class, instead
store them within their declaring class. If a closure is declared in a
function instead, store it stand-alone as before.

Closures tend to dominate among all classes, something by more than
4x. Moreover, they tend to be small and it rarely makes sense to
analyze them without their declaring class. By storing them within
their declaring class, we can cut down the total number of blobs that
need to be transferred significantly.

Note: This is only a change within HHBBC. When the bytecode is
emitted, the classes and closures will be emitted exactly as they are
before, and the runtime will see nothing different. This is purely a
HHBBC internal optimization.
