Commit title: Fix flaky test failures in ThreadPoolExecutorTest.AddPerf
Commit body:
Summary:
The test was intermittently failing because the inner `add` was executing after `stop` stopped the Executor. The fix is to hold a `KeepAlive` while the inner `add` is called, so the `stop` call blocks its shutdown process until all the `add` calls finish.

In more detail, the failure happened because of an assert failure of `DCHECK(DefaultKeepAliveExecutor->controlBlock_->keepAliveCount_ > 0)` in `DefaultKeepAliveExecutor::keepAliveAcquire`. The `stop` would decrement `DefaultKeepAliveExecutor->controlBlock_->keepAliveCount_` to `0` in `DefaultKeepAliveExecutor::keepAliveRelease`, before the `add` finished acquiring a keep alive token. Holding a keep alive in the tests ensures that `DefaultKeepAliveExecutor->controlBlock_->keepAliveCount_ > 0` while any `add` is takening place.
