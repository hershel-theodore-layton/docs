Summary:
With libc++, we may get address-comparison for RTTI objects. If the linker does not fully merge all such symbols across all loaded .so's / dylibs, we get false mismatches. It is better in the case of UniqueInstance to err on the side of false matches than to err on the side of false mismatches.
