Commit title: Partition - use sets to deduplicate DNF
Commit body:
Summary:
Without simplifications, meets and joins of DNFs can result in lots of repeated substructures. When these large, repetitive types get produced, they result in excess work for the typechecker and sometimes stack overflows.

This diff uses sets to deduplicate the DNF (e.g. `T & T -> T`, and `(T & U) | (T & U) -> T & U`) stems this runaway computation.
