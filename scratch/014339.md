Summary:
Currently we interpret `$x as E` by refining `$x` to `~E & arraykey`, because of intish and stringish casts. But we don't do this recursively e.g. `$x as ?E` refines to `?E`, which is unsound.

Fix is to do the "ish-weakening" during localization.
