Summary:
The typechecker relies on reasons for both error reporting and, in some cases, typing. Further, the error reporting implementation interacts with FIXME error silencing in a brittle way. Because of this, some of the places we want to introduce extended-reasons lead to peturbations of errors or even spurious errors.

However, in practice these spurious errors are fairly limited in scope. This diff adds a new config option (`yolo`) which lets us record the provenance on buggy paths.
