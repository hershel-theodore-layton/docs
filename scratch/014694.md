Commit title: Introduce leaf frame to WithAsyncStackCoroutine
Commit body:
Summary:
1. Introduce two new APIs (activateSuspendedLeaf and deactiveSuspendedLeaf) to "tag" suspended-leaf frames (i.e. frames for coroutines awaiting stack/executor unaware awaitables) in a way that makes them visible to debuggers.

2. Introduce a new AsyncStackFrame in WithAsyncStackCoroutine and tag it as a suspended-leaf while the coroutine is suspended. WithAsyncStackCoroutine is used to wrap awaitables that are not stack-aware (eg. folly::Future), and so we can now use debuggers to identify deadlocks caused by unfulfilled futures.

To make suspended-leaf frames visible to debuggers, we set the roots of AsyncFrames to a special value (called "cookie"). Debuggers can obtain this special value by looking for a well known symbol. They can then scan the heap-section for the cookie and find suspended leaf frames
