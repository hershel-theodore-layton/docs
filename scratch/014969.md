Commit title: Avoid one allocation in IOBufQueue::maybeReuseTail()
Commit body:
Summary:
`IOBufQueue::maybeReuseTail()` currently allocates two `IOBuf`s, one to add a reference to the original buffer, and one to take ownership of the tail.

By making the split operation first-class in `IOBuf` we can implement it more efficiently by combining the two allocations.
