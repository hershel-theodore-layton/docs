Commit title: Change representation of variadic/default parameters to support optional in future
Commit body:
Summary: Change the AAST representation of function parameters, with mutually exclusive constructors for variadic, normal (required), and optional/default parameter. So `Param_variadic` is used for `int ...$args`, `Param_optional (Some e)` is used for `int $x = 5`, `Param_required` is used for `int $x`, and `Param_optional None` will be used for `optional int $x` in a future diff.
