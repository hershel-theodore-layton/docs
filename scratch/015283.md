Commit title: fix exception-access build under FreeBSD <= 14.0
Commit body:
Summary:
X-link: https://github.com/facebook/folly/pull/2244

FreeBSD 14.1 introduced the function definition `__cxa_init_primary_exception` and introduced into `cxxabi.h` the function declarations `__cxa_allocate_exception` and `__cxa_free_exception`. Assuming them breaks the build under FreeBSD <= 14.0.

For now under all FreeBSD, just declare `__cxa_allocate_exception` and `__cxa_free_exception` and do not use `__cxa_init_primary_exception`.

Fixes: https://github.com/facebook/folly/issues/2240.
