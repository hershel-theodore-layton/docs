Summary:
Too many "sets" here made reading and writing a dizzying experience. This is the naming that I settled on:

* "atom" -- the thing that is atomic in the context of this module
* "conjunction" -- a group of atoms that are meant to be interpretted as conjuncted together
* "disjunction" -- a group of conjunctions that are meant to be interpertted as disjuncted together
* "set" / "lattice" -- a representation of a combination of atoms in DNF
