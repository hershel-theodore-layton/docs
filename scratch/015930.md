Commit title: Avoid overlapping replacements when removing adjacent annotations in codemod
Commit body:
Summary:
The replacement tooling ignores overlapping replacements, but also strips whitespace from both sides of removed annotations. This means when removing adjacent annotations, both replacements will try to remove the whitespace between them and fail.
Make each annotation own whitespace before it but not after its trailing comma to prevent this.
