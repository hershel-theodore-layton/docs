Commit title: Refactor reason construction
Commit body:
Summary:
The design of extended reasons uses two main concepts to explain the provenance of a type:

1) Flow - records a path through typing and tells us which hints and (sub)expressions contributed to the type of another expression
2) Projections - records the decomposition of a type during subtyping which lets us track how we reach a given subtype constraint.

This diff refines the flow and projections constructors and adds the following constructors
1) Axiom - this lets us record the use of user-declared information about a type. Specifically, we can records when `extends` and generic bounds were used during subtyping
2) Upper_bound & Lower_bound - this lets us 'remember' the subtyping derivation for all types in the upper and lower bound of a given type variable.
