Fix T188437747, see videos in test plan and facebook section.

The issue is not in hackfmt itself, but in the Hack language server, which shells out to hackfmt, calculates minimal edits, and sends the minimal edits to vscode.

## The Fix

- One minimal edit we sometimes apply is to replace a span of lines with `""`. In such cases, we really mean `""` and not `"\n"` and want to replace rather than appending lines. See code change in ide_formatting.ml. I admit to only 80% understanding what's going on here being only a novice in newline-handling.
- We were always passing `--range` to hackfmt, even when handling a document formatting request, even though hackfmt is perfectly happy to format an entire document (in fact, that's how `arc f`'s hackfmt integration works). This extra calculation made the logic more complex and made it harder for me to avoid breaking range formatting, so I deleted it.
