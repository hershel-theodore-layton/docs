Commit title: Fix HHBBC handling of async memoization
Commit body:
Summary:
HHBBC was conflating the two cases of memoization information being
Bottom and a wait-handle containing Bottom. A wait-handle containing
Bottom was being treated as just Bottom, which meant HHBBC inferred
the memo-cache was always empty. To fix this, store the types for the
eager and non-eager cases separately. Thus, there's no ambiguity.
