Summary:
Bug blames to: {D59028606}.

Some compilers may assume function pointers to have some minimum alignment, even as they generate functions without that alignment!

We observe gcc to generate some deleter functions with alignment less than pointer-alignment and, indeed, with no alignment at all. We do not observe clang to generate these deleter functions with alignment less than pointer-alignment.

We observe both clang and gcc to assume that functions have some minimum alignment. Experiments in: https://godbolt.org/z/bo6f7f6v6.

Fixes observed crashes in oss gcc-built fboss.
