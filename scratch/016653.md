Summary:
Must copy `errno` immediately. Anything could reset it.

There is a github issue (https://github.com/facebook/folly/issues/2141) which appears to demonstrate just such a case of `errno` being reset before its value could be logged in the assertion message.

An alternative would be to switch from `CHECK` to `PCHECK`.

Closes: https://github.com/facebook/folly/issues/2141.
