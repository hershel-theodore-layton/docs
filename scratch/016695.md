Commit title: Handle nested DebuggerEvaluationContexts
Commit body:
Summary: `DebuggerEvaluationContext`s can be nested, e.g. an expression can be evaluated when paused at a breakpoint which is hit from evaluating another expression. Nested evaluations should not reset the `DebuggerStdoutHook`. Otherwise if the outer expression outputs anything after the inner expression finishes, it won't write to the debugger stdout hook.
