Commit title: Emit metrics instead of log spam for too many reads per EVB iteration
Commit body:
Summary:
Under overload, the EVB loop iterations are becoming longer and we start limiting per-connection reads after 16. This typically happens during overload. I am not totally sure there isn't some loop scheduling problem, but even without it there is a problem of logging thousands of lines per second making logs unreadable.
Replacing the log with a metric addresses the log spam and might shed some light on whether read limiting is correlated with something particular as well.
