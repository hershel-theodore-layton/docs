Commit body:
Summary:
Enums with base `classname<T>` are currently handled incorrectly. When resolving type-constraints in sandbox mode they are treated as `arraykey` while hhbbc correctly treats them as the `Classname` type.

Their handling for reified generics is also broken but because it was consistent between sandbox and repo mode I have left it alone to avoid introducing a breaking change here. It should be fixed as a follow-up.
