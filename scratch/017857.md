Commit body:
Summary:
Remove pointless global ref `blank_tyvars` and instead pass a flag into the type printer, and also remove ref `debug_mode`. The former set type variables to print as `_` and the latter to not suppress the printing of `exact`. There was no reason to keep those separate, so add a new flag `hide_internals` to control this aspect of printing behaviour.

The refactoring made it clear that there were many places where printing was being called without blanking tyvars, even though the strings were going to into error messages. I've updated those places to hide the internals.
