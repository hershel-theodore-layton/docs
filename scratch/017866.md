Commit title: Type splat: parsing for tuple hints
Commit body:
Summary:
We now support syntax for splats inside tuples e.g. `(int, ...T)`. The restrictions are identical to splats in parameters, i.e.

* A type splat may only appear as the last element of a tuple type.
* It may not be preceded by optional or variadic elements.
* It may not have an optional or variadic (trailing ellipsis) annotation.

These restrictions ensure that the “required precedes optional precedes variadic” invariant for open tuples is preserved under expansion of a splat.

(Fixed two bugs in the Rust decl interop. See comments on files.)
