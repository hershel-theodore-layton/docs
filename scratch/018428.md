## Why

In Hack files, top-level statements are put into a function, which introduces a level of indentation. This can have ripple effects on formatting.

We autoformat on conversion to/from Hack notebooks to save users time, since they won't have to invoke the formatter or use save-to-format, or deal with a CI telling them about formatting errors.
