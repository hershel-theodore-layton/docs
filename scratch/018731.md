Commit title: Simplify IterGetKeyArr and IterGetValArr into specialized forms
Commit body:
Summary:
Sometimes the bespoke profiling information for IterInit differs from
IterGetKey/IterGetValue/IterNext (e.g. due to being weighted by the array
size). When that happens, we won't see the effects of IterInit guard during
irgen due to unprocessed preds and could emit a generic implementation of
IterGetKey and IterGetValue. The information becomes available only after
reflowing the types.

It is also possible to later learn more information about the base array due to
other optimizations.

Take advantage of that information and simplify IterGetKeyArr and IterGetValArr
into one of the specialized forms.

Reduces the amount of IterGetKeyArr and IterGetValArr instructions by ~50%.
