# What and Why?
Upon the removal of `experimental_generate_abstract_types` in the  diff to make abstract types become generally available, and ensure that only the fixtures that show the consequences of that change, rename/modify/delete the configurations that generate mutable types to match what they will eventually mean.

- In the basic fixtures
`python_experimental_generate_abstract_types` -> `python_experimental_generate_mutable_with_abstract_types`
`python_experimental_generate_mutable_types` -> `python_experimental_generate_mutable_without_abstract_types`

- In the rest of the fixtures, delete the `python_experimental_generate_abstract_types` because it's current behavior is to generate both `mutable` and `abstract` types, which will be the eventual behavior of `python_experimental_generate_mutable_types`
