Commit title: Enable structural pattern matching for thrift containers
Commit body:
Summary:
Enable structural pattern matching by setting `Py_TPFLAGS_SEQUENCE` glag or `Py_TPFLAGS_MAPPING` flag.

For regular classes, you can register them with `collections.abc.Sequence` or `collections.abc.Mapping` to enable this behavior. However, this doesn't work for Cython extension types. Cython implemented decorators like `cython.collection_type("sequence")` or `cython.collection_type("mapping")`, but these decorators are not publicly available yet.
