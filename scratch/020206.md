Commit title: do not overestimate numMethods in presence of diamond traits
Commit body:
Summary:
When loading a class, the number of methods in a class was initially estimated by `loadUsedTraits` without accounting for duplicated methods due to diamond trait inheritance.  This could incorrectly trigger the error "Class has more than 65.535 methods" (see the repro in `donotoverestimate.php` below).

This diff refines the estimation of the number of methods returned by `loadUsedTraits` so that methods imported multiple times due to diamond inheritance are counted only once.
