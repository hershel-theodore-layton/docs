Commit title: Introduce @cpp.DeprecatedTerseWrite
Commit body:
Summary:
* introduce cpp.DeprecatedTerseWrite which is a field-level annotation to follow the behavior of `deprecated_terse_write`.
* In the near future, we will place a codemod to replace all `deprecated_terse_write` option to annotation so that the information is more visible and easier to migrate field by field.
* It is intentionally created with field-level annotation with opt-out so that new fields added will be unqualified in the future.
* fixtures changes are introduced separately in the next diff to easily identify delta.
* `deprecaterd_terse_write` is always applied to an unqualified field for primitive, binary/string and container, and it is applied to structured types if it has cpp.Ref.
