Commit title: handle intersections and unions
Commit body:
Summary:
## What

For [Safe Abstract](https://fburl.com/hack-safe-abstract) features, handle unions and intersections in/of `classname` and `concreteclassname`.

Such types we now handle include the following:

`string & (dynamic | classname<C & D>)`
`classname<C> | concreteclassname<D>`, see test cases for examples.

## Why

Needed for preventing runtime errors.

Intersections and unions of/in class names appear in the wild. Especially common are `& string` and with `| dynamic` (the latter from like types).

## User-facing

no change, since Safe Abstract features are behind config `safe_abstract=true`,
