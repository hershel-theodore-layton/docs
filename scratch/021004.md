Commit body:
Summary:
In the `cython_library` macro, all `srcs` are compiled regardless of `.py` or `.pyx` suffix. That means we have unnecessary build size / time bloat due to the flat-name containers we're generating, even though they only use pure python operations!

This diff splits them out into an auxiliary `python_library` `types_auto....py`, then re-exports them via `types.py` in `-py3-types` target
