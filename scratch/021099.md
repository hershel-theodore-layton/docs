Commit title: fix nullptr-dereference in SocketAddress
Commit body:
Summary: When `getaddrinfo` fails, `SocketAddress` attempts to describe the error and throw an exception with that description. It includes the content of the `node` or `host` param, which is a C-string, without checking whether the param is nullptr. When `SocketAddress::getAddrInfo` is called with a nullptr param but where its internal call to `getaddrinfo` fails, this triggers a segv crash.
