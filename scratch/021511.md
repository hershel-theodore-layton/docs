In an effort to protect online and PSP MAPI traffic, and the reliability metrics they dictate, we will mark async, retriable traffic as one of sheddable_plus or sheddable.

**Online:** Critical mutation work resulting in necessary data or state for the caller; failures here should result in failure propagation to the caller
**PSP:** Non-critical work which can tolerate slight risk of missing data; logging, bot messaging, notifications and related work
**Async:** Work which is either expensive, or must-run but can tolerate delay; pre-computation, data repair, mass state updates

At times of system duress, dependent systems may use these priorities to DLS and CPU-cc, offloading expensive, retriable async traffic to attempt at a later time. Authors of async jobs/traffic will be able to work collaboratively with MAPI Product teams and the dependent systems to understand async job failure instances. Our online traffic will no longer need to compete with offline, async traffic within MAPI tenants on ADAL and other dependencies.

__**REQUEST PRIORTY SCHEMA**__
| Traffic | Priority |
| -- |
|  Synchronous, RFT |  critical_plus |
|  Synchronous, non-RFT |  critical |
|  Asynchronous, RFT |  sheddable_plus |
|  Asynchronous, non-RFT |  sheddable |
