## What

When generating a .ipynb file (notebook JSON) from a Hack file, ensure that:
- Every string in the "source" array (except possibly the last one) ends in "\n".
- And preserve the property introduced in D70917415 that there are no empty strings (`""`) in the "source" array

## Why

Other software we work with	has these requirements, even though VSCode can open notebooks just fine that don't meet these requirements and the requirements afaict are not in the specification: https://ipython.org/ipython-doc/3/notebook/nbformat.html
