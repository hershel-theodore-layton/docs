Commit title: Split Protocol.Value macros between stable & deprecated
Commit body:
Summary:
Split the macro generators for the `Protocol.Value` type-safe accessors between the stable boost-like APIs, e.g. `as_TYPE()`, `is_TYPE()` (`FBTHRIFT_THRIFT_VALUE_GEN_METHOD_FROM_TYPE`), and the  deprecated methods from the generated structs, e.g. `<TYPE>Value_ref()` (`FBTHRIFT_THRIFT_DEPRECATED_METHOD_FROM_TYPE`).

This allows easier removal of the relevant deprecated methods per type.
