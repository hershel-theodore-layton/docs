Summary:
The [boost-like APIs](https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[f9d8ffbaf32d446b2abeb2beeaa8015ea80d46fb]/fbcode/thrift/lib/thrift/detail/protocol.h?lines=123) for `Protocol.Object` & `Protocol.Value` are a better abstraction from the wrappers & field types for `Object` & `Value`.

They're able to remain stable while we roll out changes to the IDL/structure of these types for e.g. performance reasons D69278828

This diff focuses on the `listValue` API:
* `get_listValue()` -> `as_list()`
* `listValue_ref()` -> `as_list()`
* `set_listValue()` -> `emplace_list(...)`
* `mutable_listValue` -> `as_list()` / `move_list()`
* `move_listValue` -> `move_list()`
