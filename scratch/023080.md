Commit title: Move CheckedUnsafeCast flag to HHVM not HackC
Commit body:
Summary:
Instead of gating `CheckedUnsafeCast` in HackC, do it in HHVM, i.e. always emit the bytecode for the cast but only act on it in the interpreter and JIT when the flag is set.

Change the flag to be `Eval.CheckedUnsafeCast` instead of `Hack.Lang.CheckedUnsafeCast`.
