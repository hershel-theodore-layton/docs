Commit title: Improve JSONProtocol's writeJSONString
Commit body:
Summary:
Improve JSONProtocol's writeJSONString function by writing directly into the QueueAppender's buffer.

This trade-off greatly reduces the loop's complexity, resulting in amazing performance enhancements for aarch64 systems.

On a Grace cpu, throughput almost doubled for small strings and almost tripled for big strings.

Modern X86 systems benefit much less from the change, in fact, Grace ended up being faster than BGM on small strings:

Grace:

before:

SimpleJSONProtocol_write_SmallString                       63.85ns    15.66M
SimpleJSONProtocol_write_BigString                         18.34us    54.54K

after:

SimpleJSONProtocol_write_SmallString                       33.62ns    29.74M
SimpleJSONProtocol_write_BigString                          6.28us   159.13K

BGM:

before:
SimpleJSONProtocol_write_SmallString                       50.07ns    19.97M
SimpleJSONProtocol_write_BigString                          8.97us   111.47K

after:

SimpleJSONProtocol_write_SmallString                       47.80ns    20.92M
SimpleJSONProtocol_write_BigString                          7.27us   137.55K
