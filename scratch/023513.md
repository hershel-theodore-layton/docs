Commit title: Make AsNoexcept commute with async_closure
Commit body:
Summary:
`AsNoexcept` tasks appear in two (related) places in `folly/coro/safe`:
  - (a) `co_cleanup`  implementations
  - (b) Tasks added to simple `SafeAsyncScope` that lack result-collectors

In both scenarios, but especially in (b), it is important to let the task be implemented as an async closure. Two designs are possible:
 - Add `bool fatal_on_throw` or similar to `async_closure_config`.
 - Make sure that wrapping `async_closure` around a function making `AsNoexcept<ClosureTask<>>` Just Works.

Here, I take the latter approach since it's more composable.
