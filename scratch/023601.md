Implememt a loop-based thrift binary protocol skip algorithm for better performance.

Performance:
The performance gain mainly comes from skipping list/set/map<fixed-size-type>, also the binary/string.
With the example in the test, new impl mean time is 1.12us comparing to current 4.78us. More over, For struct with vec![i32; 1000] elements the performance can increase 28x.
However, for very simple struct, there's a slightly regression. I suspect the reason maybe changing from recursion to loop.
