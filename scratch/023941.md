# What

Make checking for calls to `__NeedsConcrete` methods via `self` and `parent` more like the checking we do for calls to `__NeedsConcrete` methods via `static.

## Why

### Soundness:
-  `self` and `parent` forward the referent of `static`, it takes only one level of indirection to make these calls just as dangerous and late-bound as calls through `static`.

### More Completeness (expressivity):

- Inside a `__NeedsConcrete` method, `static` is guaranteed to refer to a concrete class. So it's safe to call  `__NeedsConcrete` methods on `parent` (which forwards along the receiver of `static::`).

> This change is not user-facing since `needs_concrete` checking is behind a flag.
