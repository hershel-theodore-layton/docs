Commit title: Use rapidhashNano on F14<string>
Commit body:
Summary:
Switching F14's string hashing algorithm to rapidhashNano.

Benchmark on Grace improves ~25% P1827796399
Benchmark on BGM improves ~40% P1829169381

ServiceLab trials:
AMD64:

On AdRanker, improvement seems to be of ~70%, with stdCompatibleHash measuring 0.17% and rapidhashNano 0.05% https://www.internalfb.com/servicelab/experiment/5501492887/complete
If we measure on their caller, TransparentRangeHash, improvement seems to be ~75%, since cpu usage lowers from 0.2% to 0.05%.

AdFinder cuts stdCompatibleHash usage of 0.23% to almost half, with rapidhashNano measuring 0.12%: https://www.internalfb.com/servicelab/experiment/5301494349/complete
However, TransparentRangeHash only lowers from 0.23% to 0.20%, signaling an improvement of 15%

aarch64:

AdFinder cuts stdCompatibleHash of 0.20% by 40%, with rapidhashNano measuring 0.12%: https://www.internalfb.com/servicelab/experiment/5501449071/complete
TransparentRangeHash lowers from 0.22% to 0.14%, upholding the improvment.

AdRanker cuts stdCompatibleHash of 0.17% by 35%, with rapidhashNano measuring 0.11%: https://www.internalfb.com/servicelab/experiment/5401445599/complete
However, TransparentRangeHash only lowers from 0.14% to 0.12%, suggesting an improvement of 15%.
