Summary:
IIUC, it should always be fine to take `NothrowAwaitable` by-value, so there's no point in bifurcating on `must_await_immediately_v`.

With that simplification, it's also trivial to make `AsyncGenerator` promises support this form:

```
co_await co_nothrow(someNowTask());
```

The old `AsyncGenerator::await_transform` impl would try to move `NowTask`, and fail.
