Commit title: Respect growth policy when serializing external buffer
Commit body:
Summary:
D32942233 switched from cloning to multiple calls to push() in order to avoid propagating headroom/tailroom, which wastes space. Unfortunately the additional call to ensure() inadvertently bypassed the growth policy of the QueueAppender, resulting in inconsistent behavior of the serializer (where apart from this one case buffer allocations do obey the growth policy).
Using the cursor overload of push() restores the desired behavior, and also simplifies the code.
