Commit body:
Summary:
This removes logging for cases where thrift server relied or might have relied on maxQps being synced with executionRate.

This logging has served its purpose. It was meant to detect such cases over a period of time so that we could discover which services might break if we unsing maxRequests from concurrencyLimit. We have safely handled those services by opting them into the `--default_sync_max_requests_to_concurrency_limit=true` flag override.
