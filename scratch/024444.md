Commit title: `noexcept` discipline for `mustAwaitImmediatelyUnsafeMover`
Commit body:
Summary:
`mustAwaitImmediatelyUnsafeMover(std::move(awaitable))()` is the equivalent of pass-by-&& for immovable, immediately-awaitable work.

It's much easier to keep the two paths (correctly) parallel if the unsafe-mover plumbing is entirely `noexcept`.

This is de-facto true today, since we're just moving coro handles around, so we might as well enforce it as type-system invariant.
