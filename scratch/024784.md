Commit title: Standardize on snake_case
Commit body:
Summary:
There's a (not-quite-universal) trend towards `std`-style `snake_case` in `folly`.

For example, `folly/lang` largely follows that style, yfeldblum added `folly::coro` verbs like `blocking_wait`, and my recent `async_closure` & `folly::result` slithered in the same direction.

Up-stack, I was adding `folly/coro/safe/collect`, which heavily interacts with `result` and thus also became snake_case.

At this point, it felt like a preponderance of `folly/coro/safe` was getting snaked, and a small vote decided that we'll standardize the directory on snake_case now, before broad adoption.

While there's nothing wrong with `camelCase` in user code, the main motivation is that it looks more like other "system library" stuff, be it `std`, `unifex`, or the upcoming P2300 `std::execution`.

I will follow this diff with a partial rename of `NowTask -> now_task`, leaving a backwards-compat shim of `using NowTask = now_task<T>`. The idea is that simplifying `NowTask` / `now_task` adoption is more important than standardizing the spelling. In the future, we can fix this via:
  - `folly::coro::Task` -> `unsafe_task`
  - `folly::coro::NowTask` -> `task`

Future `folly/coro/safe` & related constructs will also be snaked:
  - `co_map_result`
  - `value_only_result` under `folly/result`
  - `safe_async_scope`, echoing C++26-standard `async_scope` from P3149
  - `after_cleanup`, following existing `async_closure` naming
  - `async_object`, echoing P2849
