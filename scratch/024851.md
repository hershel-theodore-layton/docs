Commit body:
Summary:
Replace usage of mutable options in `mstch_context.options` with a strongly typed `python_generator_context`.

This also enables sharing a consistent view of these options between the generator instance, Whisker prototypes and the various `python_mstch_*` nodes, while incrementally migrating from the `python_mstch_*` nodes to Whisker protoypes.

`t_mstch_generator::get_option` / `t_mstch_generator::has_option` operate on the immutable compiler options, rather than the mutable `mstch_context.options` that `mstch_base::get_option` operates on.
