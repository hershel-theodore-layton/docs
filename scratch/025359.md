Commit title: fix leak in mutable map
Commit body:
Summary:
Both `PyTuple_SET_ITEM` and `PyList_SET_ITEM` "steal" references to given element, meaning they do not increase the reference count. The caller passes responsibility for decrementing ref-count to the tuple or list.

In contrast, `PyDict_SetItem` "does not *steal* a reference" to inserted value. Hence, the caller is responsible for releasing its reference, by decrementing the ref count (via dtor of `UniquePyObjectPtr`).
