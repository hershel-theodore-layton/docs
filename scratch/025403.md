Commit title: do not modify zero copy receive IOBufs in Thrift parser
Commit body:
Summary:
FrameLengthParserStrategy by default will attempt to optimise the IOBuf passed up in readBufferAvailable(). But this is not desired for IOBufs from IoUringZeroCopyBufferPool - which should not be destructively modified in userspace without being cloned first. The optimisations causes IOBufs to no longer be page aligned, or for IOBufs to have a length of 0.

Fix by marking IOBufs from IoUringZeroCopyBufferPool as externally shared. This prevents IOBufQueue from modifying the IOBuf.
