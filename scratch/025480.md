Summary:
When a `{{> ...}}` is indented like this:

https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[0912debc38df6a8b7c662364418d685d99fef243]/xplat/thrift/compiler/generate/templates/rust/types.rs.mustache?lines=74-76

https://www.internalfb.com/code/fbsource/[d90ea9de7af59f966bb8bdb1c78301984d4eef4e]/xplat/thrift/compiler/test/fixtures/basic/out/rust/gen-rust/types.rs?lines=14-18

On the other hand, just erasing indentation from our templates makes the templates unreadable.

This diff erases the indentation but also inserts some '**=========**' separators to maintain some hierarchical structure in the templates.
