Commit title: Fix DeMorgan's theorem warnings in XLOG macro expansions
Commit body:
Summary:
Suppress clang-tidy readability-simplify-boolean-expr warnings that occur when XLOG macros expand to complex boolean expressions that trigger DeMorgan's theorem simplification suggestions.

The warnings originate from the XLOG_ACTUAL_IMPL macro which expands to:
```cpp
(!XLOG_IS_ON_IMPL(level) || !(cond))
```

There's no one-size-fits-all solution to fix this at the macro level as the optimal simplification depends on the `cond` value:

*   When `cond=true` (XLOG): becomes `(!A || false)` → simplifies to `!A`
*   When `cond=false` (XLOG_IF with false): becomes `(!A || true)` → simplifies to `true`
*   When `cond` is complex (XLOG_IF with runtime conditions): DeMorgan's applies normally

This technical complexity justifies why suppression is the most appropriate solution rather than trying to restructure the macro logic.
