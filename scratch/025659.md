Commit title: Inline functions called with concrete callables more aggressively
Commit body:
Summary:
HSL helpers such as Vec\map() are not being inlined because of their heavy
total usage divided across many call sites. They get often called with a unique
concrete callable. Inlining these helpers is profitable, as calls to these
callables become static, turning Call into a more performant CallFuncEntry.
Furthermore, these callables become inlinable as well.

Treat callees as unique functions for the purpose of inlining if the caller
passes a concrete callable, allowing HSL helpers (and other userland logic) to
be inlined more aggressively.
