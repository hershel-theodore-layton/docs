Commit title: Store resolution mismatches in the global scope
Commit body:
Summary:
In preparation for the thrift lints being added related to identifier resolution conflicts, see [Identifier resolution](https://www.internalfb.com/intern/staticdocs/thrift/docs/idl/identifier-resolution), this diff adds tracking for resolution mismatches.

As types/typedefs are replaced/rewritten throughout parsing & sematic analysis, the most correct way of catching mismatches is during the initial resolution for types.

The mismatches are stored in the `global_scope` shared by all programs, so they can easily be retrieved during the verification phase of sema.
