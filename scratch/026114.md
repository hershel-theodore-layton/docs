Commit title: `const result<V&>` is now only accessible as `const V&`
Commit body:
Summary: The original choice was for `result<V&>` to follow `result<V*>`. I changed my mind because in practice, people will want `result<V>` and `result<V&>` to quack alike, just like `V` and `V&` quack alike. The new docs give a concrete example of a safety benefit of propagating `const` in this way.
