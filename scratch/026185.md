Commit body:
Summary:
The updated `result.md` notes that `result r = std::ref(v)` now deduces `result<V&>` and not `result<std::reference_wrapper<V>>`. The key motivations:
   - `result<V&>` should be the predominant idiom (because of its ergonomics & const-safety)
   - CTAD avoids repeating the type, further improving the ergonomics
   - `result<reference_wrapper<V>>` is at best useful in edge cases, if ever.
