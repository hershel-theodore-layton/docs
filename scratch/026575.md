Commit body:
Summary:
## What

Make it so that a non-optional named param can follow optional params, since this introduces no ambiguity at the call site.

## Why

- This behavior is, in my reading, implied Hack Improvement Proposal https://fburl.com/hack_named_parameters_proposal, which specifies matching of arguments to parameters in terms of desugaring to shapes. And there is no restriction on shape construction syntax w.r.t. the order of optional vs. non-optional fields.
- This also feels like expected behavior to me, fwiw, and I can't come up with a justification for forbidding the flexibility.
