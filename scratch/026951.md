Commit title: Raise warning for bitwise ops on strings
Commit body:
Summary: We do not support bitwise operations on strings in the type-checker, but they can still be accessed dynamically. We don't support other "legacy" PHP operations on strings (e.g. arithmetic) so let's retire the bitwise ops, firstly by raising a warning. Set `Eval.WarnOnBitwiseOpInvalidType=1` for warnings, and `Eval.WarnOnBitwiseOpInvalidType=2` to produce an error.
