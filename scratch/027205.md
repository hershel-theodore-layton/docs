Commit title: Replace t_list/t_set::get_elem_type with elem_type().get_type() in Thrift compiler
Commit body:
Summary:
Replaced `x->get_elem_type()` with `x->elem_type().get_type()` where `x` is `t_list` or `t_set`.

`get_X_type` AST methods returning `t_type*` are deprecated and will be removed soon.

`get_X_type()` returns `t_type*` which is de-referenced. The corresponding `X_type()` returns `t_type_ref`, whose `operator*` de-references the contained pointer (i.e. is equivalent to dereferencing a `t_type*`.

`t_type_ref::get_type()` is also deprecated, but this unblocks the removal of `get_elem_type` and allows us to handle migrating `t_type_ref::get_type()` uniformly across the board.
