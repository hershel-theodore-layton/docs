Commit title: Update error accessors in anticipation of `rich_exception_ptr`
Commit body:
Summary:
(1) Soon, `exception_wrapper` will no longer be the implementation of `non_value_result`, so standardize its "release eptr" API on the least-common-denominator of `std::exception_ptr`.

(2) Suffix all the eptr APIs with `_slow` to discourage their use, and distinguish from similarly named accessors in non-result code.

When `rich_exception_ptr` (REP) lands, operating on raw `exception_ptr` / `exception_wrapper` will come with opportunity cost:
  - Immortal REPs are free of allocations and atomic refcounts, but translating them to std::eptr will cost.
  - The RTTI-free optimizations will be lost as well.
  - "Error annotation stacks" will be discarded in the mapping.

(3) I intend the "legacy" APIs to change as I get to the point of rewiring the folly::coro internals to use result. For this reason, I add a privacy passkey to further discourage their use.
