Commit title: avoid rcu_domain::retire invoking callbacks under a lock
Commit body:
Summary:
Calling arbitrary user-provided callbacks under a lock is a dangerous game, creating the opportunity for deadlock. The scenario is when one of the callbacks itself might somehow call `rcu_domain::synchronize()`. Strange, but possible!

Also, limiting the critical section allows other threads to wait less when calling `rcu_domain::retire()` or `rcu_domain::synchronize()`.
