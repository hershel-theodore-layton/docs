Commit title: always disable field cache for service args/return
Commit body:
Summary:
Thrift-python generates "internal" structs to hold `service` arguments or returns/exceptions. This way, it can use the same serialization/deserialization logic as normal structs.

These internal service structs only have their field accessed once! So their field cache should always be disabled.

This appears to speed-up the skynet benchmark:
