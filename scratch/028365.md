Summary:
According to [Metamate](https://www.internalfb.com/metamate/message/2106335160105079)
> Most standard CRC32 implementations (including IEEE 802.3, zlib, and many hardware-accelerated versions) use ~0U (0xFFFFFFFF) as the initial value.

Hedwig team use ~0U to compute checksums and we want to use the folly combine method to speed up the crc32 checksum for large message split into smaller chunks.

This diff makes the existing crc32c_combine method startingChecksum-aware and added unit testing to verify the correctness.
