Commit title: relaxe store-release in AsyncStackRootHolder::set
Commit body:
Summary:
`AsyncStackRootHolder::set()` has an atomic store-release on member `value` which does not synchronize-with any load-acquire. In fact, the atomic is thread-local and all accesses to it are from its owning thread.

But this can show up as a substantive cost when running test `folly/coro/test/SharedMutexTest.cpp` case `SharedMutexTest.StressTest` under TSAN.

From historical context in: {D21118885}

> This is atomic mainly to prevent compiler reordering to ensure frame pointers are appropriately visible when observed from BPF.

This seems like a good case for `std::atomic_signal_fence` (https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/atomic/atomic_signal_fence.html):

> Only reordering of the instructions by the compiler is suppressed as order instructs.

This appears to lessen the cost under TSAN.
