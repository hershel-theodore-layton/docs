Summary:
## What

**before**

needs_concrete=true hhconfig option enabled:
- The typing warning for when a __NeedsConcrete method overrides a non-__NeedsConcrete method
- All other __NeedsConcrete warnings, such as for trying to call a method that may be abstract

**after**:
- needs_concrete_override_check gates needs concrete override checks
- needs_concrete=true continues to gate all other warnings for __NeedsConcrete checks
This is a pre-existing check that was previously under the `needs_concrete` flag.

> Note: we haven't released yet, so this is not user-facing (yet)

## Why

Override checks are annoying and not always actionable because methods in non-generated code can override methods that are in generated code.

I am strongly considering launching without the override checking and only checking overrides once we can move more generated code.

Even if we do not pursue this launch strategy I suspect this flexibility may come in handy and doesn't weigh much.

## Design Choice

Neither flag implies or depends on the other.
I chose this approach because it requires the least code and seems (to me) easiest to think about.
