Summary:
Collect pre-deps while parsing units. A pre-dep is a reference to a
potential class or func, determined solely by examining the
bytecode. This is just an optimization as its used to "prime" the
scheduler and determine bundles. If we get the pre-deps right, we can
(potentially) save a round of analysis. Also add the infrastructure to
keep the pre-deps up to date when we flatten traits.
