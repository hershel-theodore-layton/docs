Commit body:
Summary:
## What

Stop Errors.try_* functions from backtracking based on the presence of warnings.

Before this change, the existence or nonexistence of a **warning** could affect **errors**, which seems wrong.

No user-facing change, since the `needs_concrete` flag has not been enabled yet and that's the only thing that seems to be affected.

## Why

The current behavior is almost certainly a bug, and, iuc, we're lucky it only affected features behind the needs_concrete flag.

My proximal motivation is to un-block launching needs_concrete=true, but this solves a more general issue with warnings-errors interaction.
