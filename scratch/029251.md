Summary:
This is the storage abstraction underlying `rich_exception_ptr`. For the overall data model & bit-discriminated union, see `docs/rich_exception_ptr.md`.

This header exports `rich_exception_ptr_separate_storage` and `rich_exception_ptr_packed_storage`, with quack-a-like interfaces.  The only difference is that one stores the union's bits separately, while the other packs them into the low 3 bits of the pointer (which are 0 due to alignment).

Up-stack, `rich_exception_ptr_impl` privately inherit from one or the other, depending on the platform.

What `rich_exception_ptr_impl` then uses is the `protected:` interface from both `rich_exception_ptr_base_storage` and in the 2 derived classes).
