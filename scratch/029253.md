Summary:
`rich_exception_ptr` is the fundamental storage type underlying the rich error system.

It is very much like `std::exception_ptr` or `exception_wrapper`, but it's implemented as a a bit-discriminated union (see `docs/rich_exception_ptr.md`) in order to support efficient, RTTI-free lookup of rich errors, to implement `constexpr` immortal errors, and a few other less-critical features.

You might ask why it's in the `:rich_error_base` target -- the reason is that the formatting implementation (up-stack) closely couples the two targets at the `.cpp` level, and there is no real benefit to trying to split the target up.

At a header level, though, `rich_exception_ptr.h` depends on `rich_error_base.h` -- and not vice versa.
