Commit title: fix potential iobuf->string truncation
Commit body:
Summary: Fix potential truncation when converting from IOBuf to string. IOBufs are a chained data structure. Logically, the IOBuf is the entire chain, rather than a specific chain element. The number of chain elements logically means nothing, it reflects the IO strategy of the producer of said IOBuf. Thus, these callsites are unnecessarily assuming the IOBuf is unchained, which can result in data truncation if they are in fact chained.
