Summary:
Adds support for capturing `$$` variables when a lambda is declared within the RHS of a pipe (`|>`) or nullsafe-pipe (`|?>`) expression.

This behavior is now gated by the feature flag `capture_pipe_variables`. A parser check now looks for illegal `$$` captures.
