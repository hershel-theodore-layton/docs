Summary:
`rich_exception_ptr` has some "special case" states:
  -  Empty eptr. Internally, this is implemented as an immortal, but due to its importance we test it separately.
  - Nothrow cancellation -- an RTTI-free bit-state intended to eventually displace throwable `OperationCancelled` to make `folly::coro` more aligned with https://wg21.link/P2300.
  - Empty `Try` -- required so that in the future we may implement `folly::Try` in terms of `result`.

These tests complement the coverage in `rich_exception_ptr_fundamentals_test.cpp`, adding the checks that aren't readily coverable in the uniform matrix.
