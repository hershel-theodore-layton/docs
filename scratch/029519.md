Summary:
The serializer.py test failed with `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named
'apache.thrift.test'`. The thrift compiler generates `apache/thrift/__init__.py`
for metadata.thrift, making `apache.thrift` a regular package. This blocks
namespace package discovery of `apache.thrift.test` from gen-python.

Changed terse_write.thrift namespace from `apache.thrift.test.terse_write`
to `thrift.test` to match other tests and avoid the conflict.
