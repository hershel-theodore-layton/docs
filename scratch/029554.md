Commit body:
Summary:
## What

Filter out needs_concrete warnings in `warnings_generated_files`.

Behavior change:
- No change from user's point of view yet, since we haven't released the relevant feature (needs_concrete=true).
- The behavior difference will be that even with `-Wgenerated` no __NeedsConcrete warnings will show in generated files, at least until we do T251491512. I think that's fine:
    - I'm the only user of `-Wgenerated` in the last 90 days: N8999515
    - Until we make a few tweaks to codegen ourselves, the warnings in generated files for needs_concrete=true are not acitonable

The change to typing_needs_concrete.ml in this diff a temporary hack to enable releasing needs_concrete=true, as I plan high-pri follow-on work to remove the filtering specific to typing_needs_concrete.ml and do general server-side filtering base warnings_generated_files.

## Context:
- warnings_generated_files is an hhconfig setting that enables assuming that files in certain directories are generated. It's a fast path to avoid checking 'generated' markers.
- needs_concrete=true controls the checks in https://fburl.com/hack_needs_concrete

##  Why

Without this change, it seems risky to me to release needs_concrete=true and generate millions of warnings that are filtered-out on the client. This may be unfounded risk avoidance, but I want a smooth launch to avoid churn for our users.

## Perf

This change is safe to land, because it makes no difference to launched features.
I did measure, though: see **Test Plan** (fb-only)

## Follow-on

In the near future, we plan to do this filtering more generally (for all warnings) on the server and can delete the non-test code in this diff: T251491512
