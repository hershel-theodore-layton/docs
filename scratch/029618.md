Commit body:
Summary:
Fundamentally what the existing functionality did was to take TLS related data
from a TLS socket and copy that data into a new socket backed by a `fd`.

This is a general purpose mechanism that is applicable to not just "stopTLS".
Any form of "do a TLS handshake, then route I/O to a socket" can benefit, such
as:
    * kTLS (After the TLS handshake, the encryption occurs in the kernel during
      read() and write())
    * PSP (Which will be integrated in following diffs)

Rename "moveToPlaintext" to "toFDSocket" and replace instances of "StopTLS"
where appropriate. Allow the caller of `toFDSocket` to specify the security
protocol for the created socket.
