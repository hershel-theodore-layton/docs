Commit title: Rename "error" to "diagnostic" wherever it can refer to error/warning
Commit body:
Summary:
## What

Sensible naming: it didn't make sense that "error" could refer either to actual errors or to warnings. Now we say "diagnostic" for error-or-warning and "error" really means "error"

## Why

Using the same word for related-but-different things was confusing and made the codebase confused, leading to a series of real bugs (D90856070, D90116914) and broken APIs I'm still fixing (D90116914) and costing us an amount of developer hours I'm too embarassed to share (since the bugs were subtle and only showed up with hh_distc, only with certain flags, and only for Safe Abstract).

## Changes

No user-facing changes.
I may have missed some spots but think this is an improvement.
Line counts increase due to formatting.
