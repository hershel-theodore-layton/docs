Summary:
UB revealed by ASAN under libc++.

In optimizeJmps, `uses` was a reference into `code.back()`. After `pop_back()`,
the reference was invalidated but still used in the `copyargs` constructor,
causing ASAN container-overflow errors. Fix by constructing `copyargs` before
`pop_back()` while the reference is still valid.
