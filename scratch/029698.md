Commit title: value_only_result requires nothrow-movability; converts to result
Commit body:
Summary:
Up-stack I introduce the idea of value-only awaitables (and pipelines), which is the coro counterpart of "noexcept functions".

*Aside:* An early draft of this notion is in `coro/AsNoexcept.h`.

A key aspect of this design is that non-throwing vocabulary (like `value_or_error` etc) returns `value_only_result<T>` where it is statically known that the result itself is infallible.

The change:
 - Makes `value_only_result` responsible for enforcing that move is infallible. This makes it much easier to program the rest of the plumbing, since it can assume nothrow movability.
 - Similarly, making `value_only_result` convertible `result` improves plumbing clarity. We only need explicit conversion for now, but it'd be defensible to make it explicit later.
