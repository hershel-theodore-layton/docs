With `non_value` renamed to `error_or_stopped`, `enrich_non_value` is no longer particularly evocative. Sure, `enrich_error_or_stopped` is clear, but ... nobody wants to read or type that.

The motivation here is that:

(1) `epitaph` evoke roughly the right image -- "annotation on the sad path". So, it alludes to both the "does not apply to happy path / values" and "explanatory text" angles of this feature.

(2) It's also a rare enough & vivid enough word that we can easily consider it "reserved" for this context.
