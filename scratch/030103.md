Commit title: preserve left-to-right evaluation semantics for named arg calls
Commit body:
Summary:
Basic idea: For codemoddability and predictability, we want to respect the written down order of argument evaluation in calls with named args. However, the naive approach (of pulling everything into tmps) is slow as it incurs lots of PushL and PopLs.

This diff introduces an algorithm that does better in common cases (when args may be statically proven to not have side effects, and when arguments are already in the right order).

We may later want to experiment with introducing new bytecodes that let you shuffle the VM stack, and use a combination of that + uninits to make things even faster, but this should be good enough to let us focus on the other aspects of the implementation.
