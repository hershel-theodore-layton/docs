Summary:
Lift pre and post increment and decrement operators to statement level. For example`
`f(++x);` becomes
```
++x;
f($x);
```
and
`f(x++);` becomes
```
$__lval_tmp_0 = $x;
$x++;
f($__lval_tmp_0);
```
