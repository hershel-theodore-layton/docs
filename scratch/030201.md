Commit title: Make SignaledFlag a sticky surprise flag
Commit body:
Summary:
SignaledFlag was one of only two non-sticky surprise flags, meaning it was implicitly cleared by fetchAndClearSurpriseFlags(). This could cause the flag to be silently dropped when handle_request_surprise() is called with a mask that doesn't include SignaledFlag.

Making it sticky ensures the flag persists until it's explicitly handled. The flag is now manually cleared in handle_request_surprise() immediately before dispatching signals via pcntl_signal_dispatch().
