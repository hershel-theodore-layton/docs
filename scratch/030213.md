Summary:
This adds the generic si_code reasons to folly signal handler. All Signals can have generic signals associated with them. Most often these indicate the signal was sent by another user-space process and not necessarily caused by a fault in the program, but there exists SI_KERNEL which indicates a generic non-specified reason for a signal.

The messages are roughly copied from my [patch to LLDB](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/commit/ff7bb17c88328276323603809d5d4549ca8bd22b)
