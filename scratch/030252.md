Commit title: Remove thrift_enable_streaming_tracking flags
Commit body:
Summary:
The `thrift_enable_streaming_tracking` ThriftFlag and `EXPERIMENTAL_thrift_enable_streaming_tracking` gflag gate whether `ContextStack` is preserved and passed to the streaming infrastructure for event handler callbacks. The ThriftFlag has been rolled out to 100% in configerator. Both flags can now be removed to make streaming tracking unconditionally enabled.

Changes:
- Remove `THRIFT_FLAG_DEFINE_bool(thrift_enable_streaming_tracking)` and `DEFINE_bool(EXPERIMENTAL_thrift_enable_streaming_tracking)` from HandlerCallbackBase.cpp (fbcode + xplat)
- Remove `isStreamTrackingEnabled()` helper and the two conditional `ctx_.reset()` blocks
- Remove flag declarations from ArtilleryStreamHandler.h and the early-return check in ArtilleryStreamHandler.cpp
- Remove flag declarations from test handler header
- Remove `TestGFlagTurnedOff` tests that tested disabled behavior
- Remove `gflags::FlagSaver` and flag overrides from all remaining tests
- Update BUCK deps to remove `//thrift/lib/cpp2:flags` dep from streaming_trace
