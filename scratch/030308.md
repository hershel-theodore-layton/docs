Commit body:
Summary:
Add stream lifecycle hooks to ClientInterceptorBase:
- internal_onStreamBegin: Called once when a stream starts
- internal_onStreamPayload: Called for each decoded payload
- internal_onStreamEnd: Called when stream completes, errors, or is cancelled

Also adds StreamPayloadInfo and StreamEndInfo structs to carry context to
each hook. Storage is passed as a separate parameter to allow the
ClientInterceptor template to unwrap it into typed RequestState*.

Also adds move constructor, move assignment, and destructor to
ClientInterceptorException for rule-of-five compliance.
