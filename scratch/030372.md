Summary:
Convert context-switching patterns in adapter templates to use
Whisker boolean checks with direct property access.

Changes:
- Replace `{{#type:typedef}}` context-switch with `{{#if type:typedef?}}`
- For typedefs, use `type:package` and `type:rust_name` directly
  (not via true_type, since true_type resolves to underlying type
  which may be a primitive without a program)
- For structured types, use `type:true_type.package` and
  `type:true_type.rust_name` since structured types have programs

cannot be migrated yet.
