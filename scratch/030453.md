Summary: Rename RocketStreamClientCallback::pauseStream() to handlePausedByConnection() and resumeStream() to handleResumedByConnection(). These methods are not driven by incoming client frames — they are connection-level backpressure signals triggered when egress buffers fill or drain. The new names make the caller/trigger explicit and distinguish them from the handle(FrameType) pattern used for frame-driven methods.
