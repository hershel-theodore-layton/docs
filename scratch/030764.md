Commit title: Reduce allocations in HTTPClientChannel::setHeaders
Commit body:
Summary:
Optimize the header encoding path in `setHeaders` to reduce heap allocations from ~4 to 2 per header containing `:`. Instead of using `folly::base64URLEncode` (which allocates a returned string) and `folly::to<std::string>` for concatenation, pre-allocate the exact size needed with `resizeWithoutInitialization` and encode directly into the buffer.

Extract the encoding logic into a standalone `encodeHeader` helper and move encoded strings into the final `add` call.
