Commit title: remove usage of std::aligned_storage
Commit body:
Summary:
`std::aligned_storage` and related types were deprecated in C++23. (See [P1413R3](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2021/p1413r3.pdf) and https://github.com/facebook/folly/issues/2321.) This PR removes usages of these types and replaces them with `folly::aligned_storage_t` or `folly::aligned_storage_for_t`.
