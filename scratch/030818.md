Summary: Asio\cancel_sleep_nothrow adds support for cancelling sleep WHs without throwing an exception. Once cancelled the WH will appear to have been completed by any observers, and they can carry on with execution. This is useful for situations where we want to cancel sleeps without the cost of throwing an exception.
