Summary:
Rewrite the benchmark suite from 2 benchmarks to 20 across 9 groups,
covering all critical code paths as a performance baseline before
upcoming queue abstraction refactoring.

Groups:
1. Single-bucket concurrent enqueue+dequeue (no limit vs with limit)
2. Multi-bucket concurrent enqueue+dequeue (100 buckets, 4 buckets, with/without limits)
3. Enqueue-only throughput (single vs multi-bucket)
4. Dequeue-only throughput (single vs multi-bucket)
5. Multiple priorities (2 and 5 priorities, single vs multi-bucket)
6. Mixed shape ({1,10} and {1,10,1})
7. Enqueue rejection under contention
8. requestCount() cost (single vs multi-bucket)
9. Pre-enqueue filter overhead

Also extracts a `runConcurrentBenchmark()` helper to reduce boilerplate
and fixes BenchmarkSuspender usage so setup is correctly excluded from
timing.
