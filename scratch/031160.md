Commit title: Fix iterator_traits for BaseIter and BaseMapIter
Commit body:
Summary:
Rename `reference_type`/`pointer_type` to the standard `reference`/`pointer`
in BaseIter and BaseMapIter. `std::iterator_traits` requires these exact names
to deduce iterator properties. The non-standard names caused compile errors
when gmock tried to instantiate matchers on MapIter, since
`std::iterator_traits<MapIter<...>>` had no `iterator_category` member.
