Summary:
A use-after-free bug was identified, where where IOBuf::wrapBuffer() created a non-owning reference to a stack-allocated buffer that could be destroyed before the async write completed.

But why allocate an `array` on the stack and `read` to it? Just create an `IOBuf` and `read` to it, then `move` it to `cb_->write()`. That avoids buffer copying, and solve the use-after-free.
