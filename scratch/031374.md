Commit title: Migrate 6 simple cpp2 program-context templates to Whisker
Commit body:
Summary:
Migrate the simplest cpp2 root templates from mstch to Whisker rendering.
These templates only depend on the Autogen macro (already Whisker-native) and use straightforward program/struct property access and loops.

For each template, the `render_to_file(prog, ...)` call in the generator is replaced with `render_whisker_file(...)`, and mstch colon-syntax properties are converted to Whisker dot-syntax equivalents:
- `program:name` → `program.name`
- `program:structs` → `program.structured_definitions`
- `program:split_structs` → `program.current_split_structured_definitions`
- `{{#program:structs}}...{{/program:structs}}` → `{{#each ... as |struct|}}...{{/each}}`
- `{{#struct:extra_namespace}}...{{/struct:extra_namespace}}` → `{{#if struct.extra_namespace}}...{{/if ...}}`

Templates migrated:
- module_metadata.h, module_metadata.cpp
- module_types.tcc
- module_types_binary.cpp, module_types_compact.cpp
- module_sinit.cpp
