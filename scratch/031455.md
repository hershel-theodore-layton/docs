Commit title: go-to-parent heuristic for classes
Commit body:
Summary:
This diff adds a new heuristic, where for every class, we add its immediate parents as successor nodes as well.

This is mostly a preparation step for CLDP heuristics, but it seems useful as a a heuristic on its own.

The new configuration option `max_class_parent_steps` determines how many consecutive go-to-parent steps we are allowed to take, independent from the general maximum distance setting.
