Commit title: address false-sharing in ThrottledLifoSem
Commit body:
Summary:
In `ThrottledLifoSem`, separate `state_` from `mutex_` and `waiters_` cachelines.

`state_` and `mutex_`/`waiters_` share a cacheline. The fast paths of `post`, `try_wait`, `try_wait_for`, and `try_wait_until` touch only state_ via CAS; the slow paths (the waking thread in `tryWaitUntilSlow`, and `maybeStartWakingChain`) modify `mutex_`/`waiters_` under lock. When the waking chain is active, fast-path threads on other cores see their `state_` cacheline invalidated by the waking thread's writes to `mutex_`/`waiters_`, causing unnecessary cross-core snooping.

Add `alignas(cacheline_align_v)` to `mutex_` so that the slow-path members land on a separate cacheline from the fast-path atomic. The waking thread pays one extra cacheline fetch, but the many concurrent fast-path threads no longer contend with it.

Identified via perf c2c on ARM SPE: loads at offset 0x0 (`state_`) were receiving data from peer caches due to stores at offsets 0x10/0x20 (`mutex_`/`waiters_`) within the same cacheline.
