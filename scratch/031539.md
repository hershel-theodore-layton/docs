Commit title: Skip crashing overflow/NaN patch test cases
Commit body:
Summary:
Skip generating conformance test cases that would trigger integer overflow (UB, caught by UBSAN) or produce NaN (uncomparable) in `PatchGenerator.cpp`.

The test generator now detects and skips:
- Integer overflow cases: adding 1 to max or -1 to min of byte/i16/i32/i64
- NaN cases: adding -inf to inf for float/double

These cases can't simply be marked nonconforming because UBSAN aborts the process on signed integer overflow before gtest gets a chance to check the nonconforming flag. Deleting them from the generator is the only way to keep the test suite green without changing prod behavior.

Also replaces `-` with `n` in test names containing negative numbers to avoid conflicting with gtest's filter syntax (where `-` separates positive from negative filter patterns).
