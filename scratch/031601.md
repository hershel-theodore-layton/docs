Commit title: Exclude compiler-consumed annotations from TypeSystem
Commit body:
Summary:
Filter out compiler-consumed annotations (thrift.Uri, scope.*) when converting
from SyntaxGraph to TypeSystem. These annotations are already structurally projected
into the type model (as the type's URI and scoping constraints) and should not
appear as runtime annotations.

This aligns the runtime TypeSystem with the serializable representation, fixing a
digest mismatch between TypeRef and SerializableTypeDefinition hashing paths.

## Why not RuntimeAnnotation

We're missing a large amount of RuntimeAnnotation markers on internal types, which is a much larger refactor. This unblocks `TypeSystemDigest` in the short term.
