Summary:
Replace atomic<uint64_t> with seq_cst ordering in
StreamInterceptorContext with a plain uint64_t counter
in the generator loop (fromAsyncGeneratorImpl). Since
each stream is driven by a single coroutine, the atomic
was unnecessary overhead on every payload.

  StreamAsyncInterceptorX1  2.84us (+15%)
  StreamAsyncInterceptorX3  3.23us (+31%)
