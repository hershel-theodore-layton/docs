Commit title: Modernize timekeeping in JobQueue to std::chrono
Commit body:
Summary:
Replace POSIX `timespec` / `Timer::GetMonotonicTime` / `gettime_diff_us` with
modern C++ `<chrono>` throughout `JobQueue` and `SynchronizableMulti`.

Changes:
- Replace `timespec` timestamps with `std::chrono::steady_clock::time_point`
- Replace duration fields (`m_dropCacheTimeout`, `m_maxJobQueuingMs`) with
  `std::chrono::nanoseconds`, standardizing on a single duration unit
- Collapse two `SynchronizableMulti::wait()` overloads
  (`long seconds` / `long seconds, long long nanosecs`) into one taking
  `std::chrono::nanoseconds`
- Fix unit mismatch bug in `dequeueOnlyExpiredImpl` where microsecond
  remainder was passed as nanoseconds to `wait()`, making the fractional
  timeout 1000x too short
- Fix missing `tv_nsec` normalization in old `wait(seconds, nanosecs)` —
  could produce invalid `timespec` for `pthread_cond_timedwait`
- Remove now-unused includes of `timer.h` and `compatibility.h`
