Summary:
Remove helper template `lib/block` that only outputs a brace.

Use Whisker brace escaping - `\{` will output a literal brace character `{`, even when followed by a tag - e.g. `\{{{#if foo?}}true{{/if}}` will output `{true` or `{` depending on `foo?` being true/false.

Adds some new-lines that were previously missing.
