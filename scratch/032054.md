Commit body:
Summary:
## What

Add validation of `--config key=value` CLI arguments in hh_client.
On invalid keys, we write to the hh client log file (see `hh --client-logname` for location). We user our pre-existing "did you mean" mechanism to make it easy to figure out what we or the user probably meant.

## Why log

End users are unlikely to see the logs. They are for helping our team during development and debugging.

We do not fail fast nor do we log warnings to the user, because invalid config keys can happen in normal workflows, due to releases and rollbacks

## Implementation notes

- Builds on the mechanism introduced in {D91687991}.
- We add a helper in ServerConfig that will be used for adding validation for hh_server later in the stack.
