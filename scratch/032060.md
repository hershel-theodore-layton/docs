Commit body:
Summary:
Add per-request observability for whether overload protection mechanisms are active on a Thrift server. Two new boolean counters are logged to the thrift_request_events Scuba table:

overload_protection_boh_enabled — whether BetterOverloadHandler (BOH) is configured with a non-zero interval and at least one resource config (CPU, memory, or network).
overload_protection_cpucc_enabled — whether CPUConcurrencyController (CPUCC) is in a non-DISABLED mode via ThriftOverloadProtectionModule.
