RegexMatchCache::RegexObject was constructing temporary std::string objects from
std::string_view on every regex match operation (operator()) and during construction.
In the hot path of prepareToFindMatches(), which iterates over all strings in the
cache, this results in O(n) unnecessary heap allocations per coalesce operation.

This diff switches to boost::regex's iterator-pair overloads:
- Constructor: boost::regex(regex.begin(), regex.end()) instead of boost::regex(std::string(regex))
- operator(): boost::regex_match(string.begin(), string.end(), object) instead of
  boost::regex_match(std::string(string), object)

Flagged by Paladin (Rule 22 - unnecessary allocations).
