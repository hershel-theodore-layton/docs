Commit title: Extend fiber stack unwinding to Apple platforms and harden tail-call safety
Commit body:
Summary:
Broaden the platform guards in BoostContextCompatibility.h from kIsLinux to kIsLinux || kIsApple, enabling frame-pointer-based stack unwinding for fibers on macOS/iOS. The SysV x86_64 and arm64 fcontext layouts are identical across Linux and Apple platforms, so the same stack-pointer extraction and frame-stitching logic applies.

Add a DISABLE_TAIL_CALLS attribute on `fiberFunc` to prevent the compiler from tail-calling into `fiberImpl->func_`, which would eliminate `fiberFunc`'s frame (`entryFrameBase_` points to unmaterialized frame) and break `fixStackUnwinding` in `deactivate`. The attribute is gated behind `TAIL_CALLS_CAN_BE_DISABLED` so that unsupported compilers gracefully skip the frame-address capture rather than silently producing a wrong value.
