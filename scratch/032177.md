Commit title: Add IsNot type_predicate variant for nonnull support
Commit body:
Summary:
Instead of adding a NonnullTag to type_tag, introduce a more general
IsNot type_predicate_ variant that negates another predicate. Nonnull
is then represented as IsNot(_, IsTag NullTag), and mixed (?nonnull) as
IsUnionOf [IsNot(_, IsTag NullTag); (_, IsTag NullTag)].

Also rename is_class_or_tuple_of_class to predicate_needs_supportdyn /
ty_needs_supportdyn and add shape support for supportdyn wrapping.
