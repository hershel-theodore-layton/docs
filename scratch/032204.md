Commit title: Remove unnecessary virtual inheritance from InlineLikeExecutor
Commit body:
Summary:
`InlineLikeExecutor` inherits from `Executor` using `public virtual`,
but virtual inheritance is only needed to resolve diamond inheritance
ambiguity. No such diamond exists — all subclasses (InlineExecutor,
QueuedImmediateExecutor, DefaultCPUExecutor) singly inherit from
InlineLikeExecutor. Confirmed by searching the entire codebase.

Removing `virtual` eliminates the hidden vbase pointer/offset overhead
and makes these types constexpr-constructible, enabling future
singleton simplifications.

Also simplified the destructor from an empty body `{}` to `= default`.
