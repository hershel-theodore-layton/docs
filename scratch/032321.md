Summary: The `namespace php` directive in Thrift files is redundant when `namespace hack` is already present, since the Hack generator ignores `namespace php` in favor of `namespace hack`. This codemod automatically strips the unnecessary `namespace php` line to reduce confusion and keep Thrift files clean. Includes a C++ codemod binary, BUCK targets, and comprehensive unit tests covering various ordering and edge cases.
