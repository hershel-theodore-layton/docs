Commit title: disallow use of `rsa::pkcs1v15`
Commit body:
Summary:
The `rsa` crate currently has a Marvin Attack CVE open for it (CVE-2023-49092).

According to https://people.redhat.com/~hkario/marvin/, one of the possible mitigations is to stop using PKCS1 v1.5, which is the specific component vulnerable to a Marvin Attack. The `rsa` docs also call this out: https://docs.rs/rsa/0.10.0-rc.17/rsa/pkcs1v15/index.html

This adds a repo-wide lint that forbids new uses of `pkcs1v15` to mitigate this CVE within our codebase.

We should be able to remove this lint once RSA v0.10 is released, which appears to reduce the risk of the timing attack to acceptable levels: https://github.com/RustCrypto/RSA/issues/19#issuecomment-3719492764

However, it's unclear when v0.10 will be released, and upgrading to v0.10 seems like it will be a tall lift given we will likely also have to update many of its dependencies (https://github.com/RustCrypto/RSA/blob/master/Cargo.toml#L17-L33), so pulling in an RC version of v0.10 doesn't seem viable (see also D100724564). Until v0.10 is released and we can revisit this, I think this lint will have to do.
