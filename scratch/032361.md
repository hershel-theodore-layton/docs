Commit title: IoUringBackend: narrow member fields to uint32_t
Commit body:
Summary:
Change six IoUringBackend member fields from size_t to uint32_t to match
the io_uring kernel ABI (all bounded by IORING_MAX_CQ_ENTRIES=65536 or
IORING_MAX_ENTRIES=32768):
- numEntries_, waitingToSubmit_, numInsertedEvents_
- numInternalEvents_, numSendEvents_

Replace numPooledIoSqeInUse_ (monotonically increasing counter) with
poolAllocRemaining_ (bounded countdown from numEntries_ to 0).

Change FdRegistry::inUse_ and constructor param from size_t to uint32_t.
Change submitBusyCheck param from int to uint32_t.
Add static_cast<unsigned int> for iov_len in RecvzcIoSqe::processSubmit.
Add static_cast<__u32> for sqIdle.count().
Widen queueRead/queueWrite nbytes from unsigned int to size_t to match
POSIX convention; narrowing to sqe->len (__u32) is centralized internally
in prepRead/prepWrite.
