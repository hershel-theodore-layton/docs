Commit title: Use Sleeper instead of yield in HazptrDomain avail_ spinlock
Commit body:
Summary: `HazptrDomain` implements a spinlock embedded in `avail_`, immediately yielding on contention. This is very aggressive given the small size of the critical section, and it can result in high latency on contention. Instead, switch to `Sleeper`, used by other folly spinlocks, which spins for a bit and then switches to sleeping yields.
