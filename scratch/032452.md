Commit title: Prevent pthread_key_delete race during shutdown by leaking keys
Commit body:
Summary:
Replace pthread_key_delete calls in `~PthreadKeyUnregister` with a shutdown flag that causes onThreadExit callbacks to no-op. This eliminates the race where pthread_key_delete invalidates keys while background threads are still calling pthread_setspecific, which was causing `EINVAL` → `std::system_error` → `std::terminate` crashes during process shutdown.

The keys and TLS values are intentionally leaked since the process is exiting and the kernel will reclaim everything anyway.
