Summary:
Add 11 tests exercising the production Connection class through MockConnection. Unlike the mock-level tests in MockConnectionTest.cpp, these verify that Connection methods correctly delegate through ConnectionHolder to InternalConnection, and that edge cases around closed connections are handled properly.

Tests cover:
- Lifecycle: initialize wires InternalConnection, close resets ok()/serverInfo()
- Delegation: serverInfo, warningCount, reusable state flow through ConnectionHolder
- Reusability logic: setCurrentSchema marks non-reusable, isReusable/setReusable no-op safely after close
- Destruction: ConnectionDyingCallback fires on destruction, does not fire after close
- Isolation: multiple connections share client but get independent InternalConnections
