Commit title: ThriftServerAsyncProcessorFactory: delegate getServiceSchema
Commit body:
Summary:
`ThriftServerAsyncProcessorFactory` wraps a service handler as an `AsyncProcessorFactory` but did not delegate `getServiceSchema()` or `getServiceSchemaNodes()`. This caused `MultiplexAsyncProcessorFactory::getServiceSchema()` to silently skip services wrapped in this factory, producing incomplete schema responses from the debug interface.

This was the root cause of the thriftdbg regression in D100663976 — services like `mast.api.read` use `ThriftServerAsyncProcessorFactory` to wrap `ServiceHandler<HpcSchedulerReadOnlyService>` on the read-only port, and the resulting MAPF schema was missing `HpcSchedulerReadOnlyService` and all its functions (including `getHpcJobs`).

The fix adds two one-line delegations to the wrapped handler's `getServiceSchema()` and `getServiceSchemaNodes()`.
