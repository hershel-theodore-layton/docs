Summary:
Adds QmuxFramer with QMUX-specific frame types that have no WT capsule
equivalent. Shared frame types (ResetStream, StopSending, MaxData, etc.)
are handled by WebTransportFramer with FrameProtocol::QMUX from the
previous diff.

QMUX-specific types added:
- QxTransportParams: parse/write with prohibited param validation and
  max_frame_size minimum enforcement
- QxConnectionClose: transport-level (0x1c) and app-level (0x1d)
- QxPing/QxPong: sequence number based
- Stream parsing with offset contiguity validation and FIN/LEN/OFF flags
