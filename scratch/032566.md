Commit title: TSimpleJson supports full precision doubles
Commit body:
Summary:
Right now, TSimpleJson loses precision, even for roundtrippable integers as casting to string only preserves the most significant 14 digits of the double, as noted in https://fb.workplace.com/groups/cpentity.users/permalink/1299903128756962/ .

17 digits is sufficient ([per wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-precision_floating-point_format#IEEE_754_double-precision_binary_floating-point_format:_binary64) referencing the standard at least) to roundtrip all doubles, but it is obviously not necessary. An optimally short roundtrippable decimal representation will require an hhvm extension or converting one of the well known dtoa algorithms (e.g. dragon4) as far as my vibed research can tell.
