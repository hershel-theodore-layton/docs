Commit body:
Summary:
Migrate distributed_ranker/lib Utils, ServerLoggerUtils, and tests from the deprecated `apache::thrift::for_each_field` visitation API to the modern `apache::thrift::op::for_each_field_id` API.

Changes:
- Utils.h: Replace `_for_each_field.h` and `_visitation.h` includes with `thrift/lib/cpp2/op/Get.h`. Migrate `copyExtractedFeatureIds` two-struct visitation to `for_each_field_id` with `op::get<Id>`.
- Utils.cpp: Migrate `updateWriteBackEntity` (single-struct with field ID/name checks), `mergeFeatures` (two-struct for ExtractedFeatures and CustomizedExtractedFeatures), and `merge` (two-struct for MetaInfo). Move template specializations of `mergeImpl` before `mergeFeatures` to avoid implicit-instantiation-before-explicit-specialization errors.
- ServerLoggerUtils.cpp: Migrate `logEntityAndFlush` and `logCacheEvictionAndFlush` single-struct visitation.
- tests/UtilsTest.cpp: Migrate two `for_each_field` assertions in FilterForFeatureIds and CopyExtractedFeatureIds tests.
- BUCK: Replace `distributed_ranker_data-cpp2-visitation` dep with `thrift/lib/cpp2/op:get`. Remove `metadata-cpp2-types` dep. Add `op:get` dep to `logger_utils` and test targets.

Also migrates `thrift/test/reflection/visitation_for_each_include_order_test` as a drive-by.
