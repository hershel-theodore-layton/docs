Commit title: Mark NewRClsMeth/NewRFunc Vec source as MR (MovesReference)
Commit body:
Summary:
NewRClsMeth and NewRFunc transfer ownership of the reified generics Vec
to the newly allocated RClsMeth/RFunc pointer without incrementing the
refcount. The Vec source must be marked MR so that optimizeRefCounts
correctly models the ownership transfer and does not elide the IncRef
that keeps the Vec alive for subsequent uses.

Without MR, optimizeRefCounts treats NewRClsMeth as a non-consuming use
of the Vec, concludes the IncRef before it is redundant, and removes it.
When the callee later frees the RClsMeth (which contains the Vec), the
Vec is freed, and subsequent uses of it are use-after-free.
