Summary:
Add a `for...else` clause to `Union.fromValue()` that emits a `RuntimeWarning` when the given value does not match any arm of the union. The warning includes the value, its type (module + qualname), and a notice that this will become a `TypeError` in the future.

This is a deprecation-style warning to surface silent no-ops where callers pass an incompatible type to `fromValue` and get back an empty union with no indication of failure.
