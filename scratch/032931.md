Commit title: Guarantee within-stripe submission order in StripedEDFThreadPoolExecutor
Commit body:
Summary:
`folly::EDFThreadPoolExecutor` breaks deadline ties by submission order. `folly::StripedEDFThreadPoolExecutor` does not, and providing the same guarantee globally would defeat the purpose of striping (it would require cross-stripe synchronization on every enqueue). However, providing it _within_ a stripe is essentially free, since enqueues already go through `DistributedMutex::lock_combine`.

Add a per-queue counter that is bumped under the same critical section as the heap insertion, and use it as a tiebreaker in the heap comparator. Document the new guarantee in the class doc.
