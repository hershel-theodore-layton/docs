Commit title: Optimize coeffect checks at the VASM level
Commit body:
Summary:
Rewrite the ARM64 shifted bit-test sequence emitted for coeffect checks at the VASM layer. After lowering, the prologue produces `shrqi` + `xorqi` + `testqi` against the shifted operand; the simplifier now folds those into `xorqi64` + `testqi64` against the original pre-shift source using a full 64-bit logical-immediate mask. The now-dead `shrqi` is then sunk to its use site by `sinkDefs`. This shortens the fast path and avoids materializing the shifted operand.

Adds two new ARM64-only VASM instructions, `testqi64` and `xorqi64`, that accept full 64-bit logical-immediate masks; the existing `testqi`/`xorqi` forms only accept the smaller `logical_imm` range. The fold-imms-arm pass picks them when an operand is a logical bitmask rather than a small immediate.
