Commit title: Remove redundant @thrift.Uri overrides in php_hack_ns fixture
Commit body:
Summary:
The php_hack_ns fixture had explicit `thrift.Uri` annotations on every
type that pinned URIs to the file-path-derived form
(`facebook.com/thrift/compiler/test/fixtures/php_hack_ns/src/module/<Name>`),
overriding the package-derived form that the declared
`package "test.dev/foo/php/ns"` would otherwise produce.

Removing the overrides lets the URIs flow from the package declaration,
which is the natural behavior the fixture should be exercising. URIs in
generated output flip from `facebook.com/.../module/<Name>` to
`test.dev/foo/php/ns/<Name>`.

Also drops the now-unused `include "thrift/annotation/thrift.thrift"`
and the corresponding stale `thrift_types.php` files in the fixture's
`out/` directory.
