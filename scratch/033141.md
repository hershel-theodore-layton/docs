Summary:
Adds `test_stream_request_context_invalid_after_request` to `test/stream.py`, which reproduces a heap-use-after-free on trunk when `_enable_validity_enforcement()` is not called.

The test captures a `RequestContext` in a streaming handler, pauses the generator, then sends 11 dummy requests (with `set_io_worker_threads(1)`) to evict the stream request's `DebugStub` from `RequestsRegistry`'s finished list, freeing the underlying `Cpp2RequestContext`. Without the invalidator, accessing `priority()` at this point dereferences freed heap memory (`ASAN: heap-use-after-free` at `Cpp2RequestContext::getCallPriority()`). With `_enable_validity_enforcement()`, the access safely raises `RuntimeError` instead of segfaulting.
