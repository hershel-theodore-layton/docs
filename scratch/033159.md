Commit title: Replace raw `throw std::xxx` with `folly::throw_exception<>` in io headers
Commit body:
Summary:
Three raw `throw std::xxx()` call sites in `xplat/folly/io/Cursor.h`,
`Cursor-inl.h`, and `IOBufQueue.h` are migrated to
`folly::throw_exception<>()`. Behavior under exceptions-enabled is
identical (folly::throw_exception unwraps to a real throw); under
-fno-exceptions it lowers to `std::abort()`, which is the only viable
behavior for an inline throw in a header the consumer's TU compiles.

The mirror at `fbcode/folly/...` carries identical changes (dirsync).
