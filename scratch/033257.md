Summary:
Adds a solver to determine types from accumulated constraints. This works by:
1) Converting structural constraints to types. When `as_data` is true, can_index constraint indexed by strings become open shapes and those indexed by ints become vecs. When its false, we use KeyedContainer

2) After all structural constraints are eliminated, we take the intersection of upper bound type constraints

3) Finally, subsitute all shadow tyvars for their solutions
