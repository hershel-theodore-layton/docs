Instead of LOG(FATAL) after reap timeout, it should force close the connection.

FastHandlerCallback will take a DelayedDestructionGuard on the app adapter to keep just the app adapter alive until it's done. The conneciton itself will close, and everything else would be destroyed.

When the FHC calls writeResponse on App adapter, it would realize that the connection is closed, and just drop the response and bailout.
