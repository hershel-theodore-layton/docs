Commit title: StopTLSv2: fix slicing predicate
Commit body:
Summary:
The predicate to decide whether Fizz will slice writes or not only checked the head IOBuf, but slicing will be done across the chain. This meant that a small non-tagged IOBuf in front of the large StopTLSv2 record will cause the StopTLSv2 record to be sliced.

Fix by unconditionally disabling slicing on StopTLSv2 configured connections.
