Commit title: Remove linear-scan overload from `folly::contains`
Commit body:
Summary:
Remove the `folly::contains` overload that accepted non-associative containers (vectors, arrays, strings) and performed an O(n) `std::find` scan. The remaining overload requires containers with `.contains()` (sets, maps, etc.), giving `folly::contains` a uniform sub-linear time complexity.

Callers that were relying on the linear-scan fallback need to migrate to `::ranges::contains` (from range-v3) or `std::find` directly.
